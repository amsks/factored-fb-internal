\paragraph{Notes.}
$^{\ast}$ OOD-goal split: training goals confined to block-$x<0$, standard (full-space)
eval. $^{\dagger}$ Repaired protocol: \texttt{obs}/\texttt{goal\_mask\_dims} $=[36{:}39]$
(the baked-in goal coordinates masked out of observations \emph{and} goals),
\texttt{p\_randomgoal}$=0$, and a \emph{per-episode synthesized} eval goal --- the
episode's own target written into the masked goal frame, replacing the old loader's single
fixed frame (the highest-reward \texttt{next\_observation} in the whole dataset), which fed
every goal-conditioned critic the same wrong goal.

$^{b}$ \emph{Bimodal}: two of three seeds collapse (\texttt{cube-double} $d512$:
$0.000/0.392/0.000$; \texttt{mw-push} $d50$: $0.30/0.32/0.76$), so mean $\pm$ sd is a poor
summary of a two-mode distribution rather than a noisy middling score. Note that
\texttt{mw-push} $d50$ sits \emph{below} its own BC floor.

$^{c}$ The unmasked (goal-visible) BC control reaches $0.967\pm0.031$ on
\texttt{ms-pick-cube} and $0.980\pm0.020$ on \texttt{ms-push-cube}. The masked pick-cube
floor is a contact-precision limit rather than an evaluation artifact, verified by
checkpoint re-evaluation under the corrected grasp-offset synthetic goal frame
($0.02$--$0.04$, control $0.98$). \texttt{ms-push-cube}'s goal is determined by the cube
pose, so goal-blind BC saturates and critic guidance can only subtract. At 1M and at
cube-matched structure, ManiSkill therefore supplies no architecture-discriminating cell in
either direction.

\emph{\texttt{cube-double} discriminators.} Widening the kernel's informative band moves
the two critics in opposite directions: $\gamma$ $0.99\to0.995\to0.999$ takes GCIQL
$0.377\to0.560\pm0.012\to0.632\pm0.066$ (its best two-cube numbers, confirming the task
really was horizon-limited) while biGCIQL $d50$ falls
$0.257\to0.113\pm0.196^{\,b}\to0.000\pm0.000$. Also at $\gamma=0.99$: CRL
$0.015\pm0.002$, GCIQL$+$qb75 $0.381\pm0.062$.

\emph{E1 size ladder} --- the one sanctioned sub-1M content, because it is \emph{about}
dataset size; whole-trajectory truncations of the \texttt{cube-single} play pack, reported
as prose rather than a second table. At 48k/100k/250k transitions: BC
$0.052/0.055/0.072$, GCIQL $0.315/0.508/0.704$, biGCIQL $d50$ $0.343/0.701/0.904$, against
the 1M row above ($0.09$ / $0.732$ / $0.927$). The edge is \emph{absent} at 48k (inside one
seed-sd), half-formed at 100k, at full strength by 250k, and barely moves after --- it does
not exist below ${\sim}10^5$ transitions. That is the reason for the 1M-only policy: down
in the 48k--100k band, ``no architectural edge on this benchmark'' and ``not enough data
here to form one'' produce the same table, and every quarantined Metaworld/ManiSkill arm
lived in exactly that band. The truncation knob is a verified no-op at full size
(single-seed \texttt{full}: $0.908$).

\emph{density-hard context.} The \texttt{cube-single} anchor matches its reference
implementation ($0.916$ vs.\ bridge $0.915$; the soft variant $0.892$ vs.\ bridge $0.929$),
which is what certifies the port and licenses the column; its \texttt{mw-pick-place}
failure is therefore not a hyperparameter accident --- it survives the pre-registered
\texttt{p\_randomgoal}$=0.3$ ablation ($0.140\pm0.150$). (\texttt{td\_fb} phase 1 certified
at $0.952$ single-seed vs.\ bridge $0.967$; phase 2 \textsc{cancelled 2026-08-12}, and no
\texttt{td\_fb} result is claimed anywhere.)

\emph{Mechanism eliminations} (\texttt{cube-single}, read against the $0.732$ / $0.927$
pair): bounding the monolithic critic's outputs ($\kappa\tanh(\cdot/\kappa)$, $\kappa=75$)
recovers at most $3$ of the $19.5$-point gap ($0.760\pm0.037$), so boundedness is not what
bilinear is buying; removing the $\psi$-LayerNorm leaves bilinear essentially where it was
($0.909\pm0.032$), so norm concentration is not load-bearing either; and $d{=}15$ already
gives $0.837\pm0.027$ ($d$ bracket $10\to0.62$, $15\to0.84$, $20\to0.92$; saturated by
$d\approx20$), so the useful latent dimension is small. What survives both eliminations is
the ranking-coherence account.

\emph{Provenance.} Every dataset is one packed \texttt{npz} per task of ${\sim}1{,}001{,}000$
transitions. OGBench rows are stock ($1000\times1001$). Metaworld \texttt{play-resample} v3
is $2000\times500$ from \texttt{data\_collection/collect\_mw\_play\_resample.py}, changelog
in \texttt{hf\_dataset/README\_play.md}; \texttt{play-noise-hard-1m} is $10000\times100$,
collected 2026-08-12. \texttt{ms-play-resample} is $8000\times125$ for pick ($11.0$
relocations per trajectory, dwell $0.279$, cube-matched structure) and $20000\times50$ for
push ($1$ relocation per trajectory, entered as a declared control). Quarantined sub-1M
packs --- the $2.5\times10^4$ ManiSkill packs, the $10^5$ Metaworld packs, the
$4.8\times10^4$ upstream ManiSkill pack, the $2\times10^5$ Box2D packs --- and every result
trained on them live only in git history.
