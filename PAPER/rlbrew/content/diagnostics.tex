\section{Diagnostic Method}
\label{sec:method}

We now turn the interaction-conditioned view from Section~\ref{sec:theory} into diagnostics. 
The goal is to separate failures of coarse reachability from failures that occur after control has been acquired, when the policy must preserve and exploit the post-interaction regime.

\subsection{Evaluation setting}

We study the state-observation version of \ogbench{} \task{} over five held-out evaluation tasks, denoted \textit{task1} - \textit{task5}.
State observations remove visual encoder capacity as the main confound and isolate failures in the learned control representation.
Our completed method set contains four state-based URL methods: \gciql{}, \crl{}, \fb{}, and \rldp{}.
We report interquartile mean (IQM) success with stratified-bootstrap confidence intervals for aggregate performance.
We then analyze deterministic closed-loop rollouts using the phase diagnostic below.

\subsection{Phase decomposition}

We instantiate the sets from Section~\ref{sec:theory} using the state-only thresholds in Table~\ref{tab:failure_criteria}.
Each rollout is successful if all criteria are satisfied; otherwise, it is assigned to the first failed criterion in the ordered pipeline.
This converts scalar success into a phase-resolved failure profile.

\begin{table}[htb]
\centering
\caption{Operational criteria for assigning the first failure phase in state rollouts.}
\label{tab:failure_criteria}
\begin{tabular}{ll}
\toprule
\textbf{Phase event} & \textbf{Criterion} \\
\midrule
Approach reached  & Minimum gripper-to-cube distance $<6$ cm at any point in the episode \\ \addlinespace
Control acquired  & Cube lifted $>3$ cm above table with gripper closed for at least $5$ consecutive steps \\ \addlinespace
Maintain control  & Final cube height above table $\geq 3$ cm \\ \addlinespace
Transport success & Final cube pose within $5$ cm of the goal pose \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The criteria are mechanical and policy-agnostic.
They do not use the value function, learned representation, model rollout, contrastive score, or successor-measure estimate.
The same decomposition therefore applies to goal-conditioned, contrastive, predictive, successor-measure, and model-based methods.

\subsection{Cross-method failure profile}

For each method, we report the percentage of episodes assigned to success, approach failure, grasp failure, maintain-control failure, and transport failure.
This distribution distinguishes methods that fail before control acquisition from methods that acquire control but fail to preserve or exploit it.

We also report the post-contact share of failures:
\begin{equation}
\text{Post-contact share} = \frac{\text{Maintain fail}+\text{Transport fail}}{100-\text{Success}}.
\end{equation}
This quantity asks, conditional on failure, how much failure mass appears after control has been acquired.
