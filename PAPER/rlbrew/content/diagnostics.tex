\section{Diagnostic Method}
\label{sec:method}

We now turn the interaction-state hypothesis from Section~\ref{sec:theory} into a rollout-based analysis.
The goal is to separate failures during approach and control acquisition from failures during maintain control and transport.

\subsection{Evaluation setting}

We study the state-based version of \ogbench{} \task{} over five held-out evaluation goals, denoted \textit{task1} - \textit{task5}.
Using state observations removes visual encoding from the comparison and focuses the analysis on the learned values, representations, and policies.
We evaluate five state-based URL methods: \gciql{}, \crl{}, \fb{}, \rldp{}, and \psm{}.
We report interquartile mean (IQM)~\citep{agarwal-neurips21a} success with stratified-bootstrap confidence intervals for aggregate performance.
We then analyze deterministic closed-loop rollouts using the phase decomposition below.

\subsection{Phase decomposition}

We instantiate the sets from Section~\ref{sec:theory} using the simulator-state thresholds in Table~\ref{tab:failure_criteria}.
Each rollout is successful if all criteria are satisfied; otherwise, it is assigned to the first unmet criterion in the ordered sequence.
This converts aggregate success into a phase-specific failure profile.

\begin{table}[htb]
\centering
\caption{Operational criteria for assigning the first unmet phase in state-based rollouts.}
\label{tab:failure_criteria}
\begin{tabular}{ll}
\toprule
\textbf{Phase}      & \textbf{Criterion} \\
\midrule
Approach reached    & Minimum gripper-to-cube distance $<6$ cm at any point in the episode \\ \addlinespace
Control acquired    & Cube lifted $>3$ cm above the table with the gripper closed for at least $5$ consecutive steps \\ \addlinespace
Maintain control    & Final cube height above the table $\geq 3$ cm \\ \addlinespace
Transport success   & Final cube pose within $5$ cm of the goal pose \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The criteria are computed directly from simulator states and applied identically to every policy.
They do not depend on a method's learned value, representation, or model estimate.
The same decomposition can therefore be applied to goal-conditioned, contrastive, predictive, successor-measure, and model-based methods.
These thresholds are task-specific operational criteria: maintain control is a final-state criterion and does not require uninterrupted control throughout the remainder of the episode.

\subsection{Cross-method failure profile}

For each method, we report the percentage of episodes assigned to success, approach failure, control-acquisition failure, maintain-control failure, and transport failure.
This distribution distinguishes methods that fail during approach or control acquisition from methods that acquire control but fail during maintain control or transport.
We also report the post-acquisition share of failures:
\begin{equation}
\text{Post-acquisition share} = \frac{\text{Maintain fail}+\text{Transport fail}} {100-\text{Success}}.
\end{equation}
This quantity measures the fraction of failed episodes in which the control-acquisition criterion was satisfied before the rollout failed.
