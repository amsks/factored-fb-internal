\paragraph{Notes.}
$^{\ast}$ OOD-goal split: training goals confined to block-$x<0$, standard (full-space)
eval --- the deliberately induced goal-coverage failure. $^{\dagger}$ Repaired protocol:
\texttt{obs}/\texttt{goal\_mask\_dims} $=[36{:}39]$ (the baked-in goal coordinates masked
out of observations \emph{and} goals), \texttt{p\_randomgoal}$=0$, and a \emph{per-episode
synthesized} eval goal replacing the old loader's single fixed frame, which fed every
goal-conditioned critic the same wrong goal.

$^{a}$ Pooled over nine \texttt{bcflow} runs ($n{=}9$: three critic conditions $\times$
three seeds; the \texttt{bcflow} actor ignores the critic, so every such run is the same
BC control).

$^{b}$ \emph{Bimodal/wide seeds}: \texttt{mw-push} on \texttt{play-resample} v3 is
$0.30/0.32/0.76$ --- a two-mode distribution sitting \emph{below} its own BC floor ---
and on \texttt{play-resample-hard} v3 it is $0.48/0.66/0.74$, the same instability in
milder form; mean $\pm$ CI is a poor summary of both.

$^{c}$ The unmasked (goal-visible) BC control reaches $0.967\pm0.035$ on
\texttt{ms-pick-cube} and $0.980\pm0.023$ on \texttt{ms-push-cube}. The masked pick-cube
floor is a contact-precision limit rather than an evaluation artifact (checkpoint
re-evaluation under the corrected grasp-offset synthetic goal frame reproduces
$0.02$--$0.04$, control $0.98$); \texttt{ms-push-cube}'s goal is determined by the cube
pose, so goal-blind BC saturates and guidance can only subtract. Neither cell
discriminates architectures.

$^{d}$ \emph{Provenance of the Density-FB OGBench cells}: pasted from the
method-development (bridge) stack --- reference implementation, FQL$+$BC flow actor,
\emph{hard} variant, same stock 1M packs, 500k steps; \texttt{cube-double} ($0.700/0.648$)
is an $n{=}2$ cell. This repo's port reproduces
\texttt{cube-single} at $0.916\pm0.016$, which is what certifies the port. NOTE the config
seam: the three stock-OGBench rows are hard-variant reference numbers, while the OOD-split
row and every row below runs this repo's frozen \emph{soft} config (footnote $e$); a soft
rerun of the three stock rows would unify the column. The Metaworld Density-FB cells are this
repo's port under the repaired protocol (the \texttt{mw-pick-place} failure survives the
pre-registered \texttt{p\_randomgoal}$=0.3$ ablation, $0.140\pm0.170$).

$^{e}$ \emph{Frozen-config column (2026-08-17).} Every non-OGBench Density-FB cell (and the
OOD-goal split) is a fresh 3-seed run under ONE frozen config: the \emph{soft} variant
(\texttt{measure\_density\_hard=false}), all else stock. This config was selected on the
\texttt{play-resample} \texttt{mw-pick-place} dev cell after the original hard-variant
column collapsed there ($0.133\pm0.057$, a diagnosed actor-bootstrap training instability,
not a capacity limit); soft graduated all three screen seeds ($0.80/0.82/0.82$, drops
$\le0.10$). Residual seed spread persists on \texttt{play-resample-hard}
\texttt{mw-pick-place} ($0.90/0.52/0.60$) --- the same instability in attenuated form ---
so mean $\pm$ CI remains a wide summary of that cell.

$^{f}$ FB OGBench cells: external provenance (reference-stack numbers, not this repo's
runs); \texttt{cube-single} is a 10-seed mean reported without a CI.

$^{g}$ FB non-OGBench cells: this repo's port under the repaired protocol, rerun 2026-08-17
after fixing two FB-only harness bugs (training-side goal-channel masks were silently inert
for \texttt{FBDataset}, and eval conditioned on a zero $z$; both fixed, all cells rerun ---
the pre-fix runs scored $0.000$ everywhere). Caveat: FB's goal sampling is its own
\texttt{train\_goal\_ratio} $z$-mix rather than the shared GCDataset goal mix, so exact
goal-sampling parity with the other columns is structurally unattainable for this baseline.

\paragraph{How each dataset was generated.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{cube-single} / \texttt{cube-double} / \texttt{scene} /
($1000\times1001$ transitions), collected by OGBench's own scripted noisy plan-based
oracles; downloaded, not collected here. Standard fixed \texttt{task\_infos} eval.
\item \texttt{cube-single} OOD-goal split: the same stock \texttt{cube-single} pack;
during training, goal relabeling is restricted to goals with block-$x<0$; eval is the
standard full-space protocol.
\item Metaworld \texttt{play-resample} v3:
\texttt{data\_collection/collect\_mw\_play\_resample.py} --- $2000$ trajectories
$\times\,500$ wrapper steps of task-agnostic play; the scripted Sawyer oracle chases a
target re-drawn from the env's own goal space every $44$ steps on a timer (never on
success), matching OGBench play; \texttt{eps\_max}$=0$, \texttt{noise\_max}$=0.02$;
trajectories whose puck escapes the reachable workspace are rejected and re-rolled;
${\sim}12$ relocations per trajectory, ${\sim}24$k distinct goals. Changelog in
\texttt{hf\_dataset/README\_play.md}.
\item Metaworld \texttt{play-resample-hard} v3: identical collector and relocation
chaining, \texttt{noise\_max}$=0.1$.
\item Metaworld \texttt{play-noise-hard-1m}:
\texttt{data\_collection/collect\_mw\_standalone.py} (\texttt{play-noise} regime) ---
$10000$ episodes $\times\,100$ steps, one target per episode (no re-draws),
\texttt{expert\_frac}$=0$, hot mixture (\texttt{eps\_max}$=0.5$ random-mix,
\texttt{noise\_max}$=0.3$ Gaussian); collected 2026-08-12; ${\sim}10$k distinct goals.
\item ManiSkill \texttt{ms-play-resample}:
\texttt{data\_collection/collect\_ms\_play\_resample.py} --- \texttt{ms-pick-cube}
$8000\times125$ with in-episode target re-draws ($11.0$ relocations per trajectory, dwell
$0.279$, cube-matched structure); \texttt{ms-push-cube} $20000\times50$ with $1$
relocation per trajectory, a declared structure-mismatched control (its goal is determined
by the cube pose). Goal dims masked at training and eval.
\end{itemize}
