\caption{\textbf{A deterministic MDP where global spherical FB approximation loses control.}
Left and Right move between adjacent states; outward actions at the endpoints
stay in place. The agent starts at $s_0$ and receives $+1$ on entering $s_1$
(Goal), which is continuing. Successors use a fixed uniform Left/Right policy,
$\gamma=0.8$, uniform state-action sampling, and its natural uniform reference.
At dimension two, with $\|B(s)\|_2=\sqrt2$, two FB forward heads, and backward
orthogonality coefficient $1000$, every global regularized squared fit to the
exact successor target prefers Left at Start: its normalized Right-minus-Left
value gap is at most $-0.02875$, whereas the true gap is $11/85$.
A feasible spherical rank-two FB representation preserves all values for this
goal reward, so the failure occurs despite sufficient task capacity.
Every global rank-two-logit categorical fit induces optimal control from Start,
with return $25/9$, while the FB fit stays at Start and earns zero.
These are exact-target approximation results with exact reward integration,
not convergence claims for joint TD or learned actors.}
