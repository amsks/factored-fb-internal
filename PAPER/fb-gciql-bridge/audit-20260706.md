## 1. Interpolation IQL ↔ measure: attempted, 18/18 crashed, scale-confounded

The knob exists and is healthy in code: `total_loss` (`gciql_flow.py` ~line 593) adds
`measure_coef · measure_loss` **on top of the full IQL loss** when `measure_coef>0` and
`measure_only=False`. Summaries of the mc* runs show both `critic/value/*` and `measure/*`
metrics — confirming additive.

**Every run crashed:**

| Family | coef | crash step | state at crash |
|---|---|---|---|
| `GCIQL-Bil-MeasureSA-mc{0.3,1,10}` (critic legs) | any | **≤15k** | success 0, grad_norm ~1.7k |
| `GCIQL-Bilinear-Measure-mc0.3` (older cfg, legs key absent) | 0.3 | ~110–130k | success 0–0.008 |
| `…-mc1.0` | 1.0 | ~305–315k | success ~0.06, q_mean 38.6, critic_loss 104, grad_norm 1.4k |
| `…-mc10` | 10 | ~330–340k | success ~0.05, **q_mean 125, q_max 512, critic_loss 1441, grad_norm 12.4k** |

Reference scale (healthy anchor): critic q_mean −19, q_max 1.5, critic_loss 1.06,
grad_norm 41.

**Mechanism:** the measure gradient inflates the shared critic scale until explosion. This
is the same unbounded ⟨F,z⟩ inflation documented on the torch side (the ISFB AWR std-norm
fix): F grows without bound while nothing caps it. It is systemic across the JAX measure
family — MeasureOnly / C4 / FBz / D1 all run at **actor q_mean 2,589–4,838** with grad_norms
12k–24.5k; one MeasureOnly seed crashed @225k while holding its group's best success (0.144).

**Consequence:** "what does adding a successor measure to a working IQL critic do?" is an
open question, and a naive rerun will crash again. A valid rerun needs **scale control**
(see spec): small λ, measure loss normalized by `sg[|M|]`-style factors (the actor's Q-term
already uses exactly this trick), and/or stop-grad variants that quarantine the measure
gradient to ψ or a residual head.

## 2. SF-feature-TD campaign: semantic confound — headline claim not supported

### The confound
`sf_loss` trains `φ(s,a) ← b(s) + γ·mask·φ_target(s',a')` and the actor goes greedy on
`Q = ⟨φ(s,a), ψ(g)⟩/√d` with ψ = identity (b(s)=s, d=28) or frozen-random (d=50).
**`batch['rewards']` is never used anywhere in the SF pipeline.** The implicit reward being
optimized is therefore `r(s) = ⟨s, g⟩/√d` (identity) — a **linear** function of the state
whose maximizer is *not* "reach g" but "push every obs coordinate as far as possible in the
direction of g." Overshooting beats arriving. A *perfectly fit* SF critic yields the wrong
policy. Frozen-random features have the same defect (arc-cosine-kernel similarity grows with
state norm; not peaked at s=g).

The confound was **known at config time** —
`configs/gciql/cube_single_state_gciql_flow_sf_feature_td.yaml:7` says
*"(confounded -- identity b makes reward linear g.s)"* — but was dropped from the
conclusions in PROJECT-MAP §③ and the handoff memories.

### Why the reference comparison ("reproduces the known canonical-SF collapse") fails
Verified in the reference repo (`…/scratchpad/simple_successor_features/agent/`):
- Their domain is **DMC locomotion with dense rewards** that are ≈linear in state
  coordinates *by construction* (walker-run reward ≈ a velocity coordinate). The linear
  readout premise is **valid there** and invalid for sparse goal-indicators on cube.
- Their **working** variant has an explicit reward loop: `sf_simple.py:322`
  `reward_prediction_loss = F.mse_loss(predicted_reward, reward)` + scalar Q-TD (`:357`).
  **CORRECTION (2026-07-10, re-read from source):** line 322 fits the **task vector** `z` (a free
  `nn.Parameter`), not the basis — `next_rep` is computed under `torch.no_grad()` and only
  `task_opt` steps. The basis is trained by the scalar critic TD. Their reward model is thus
  ASYMMETRIC (`⟨φ_basis(s',a'), z⟩`, net × free vector) and has no ψ=0 saddle; our symmetric
  `⟨ψ(s),ψ(g)⟩` does. And `:357` is a **scalar** backup `r + γ·min(Q1',Q2')` — the working method
  contracts with z FIRST and never runs a TD on the representation.
- ~~Their collapsing baseline (`sf_canonical.py`) at least optimizes the right reward and
  collapses; ours optimizes the wrong reward~~ — **WRONG, retracted 2026-07-10.** `sf_canonical`
  has **no reward term at all**: `reward` is an unused argument of its `update_critic`, whose
  target is `basis_features + γ·target_sf` with `basis_features = F.normalize(obs)` — the raw
  observation. Its implicit reward is `⟨normalize(s), z⟩`, the *same* linear proxy we called a
  confound. The reference's own canonical baseline shares our identity-base defect, so the
  comparison is closer than this audit assumed. Ours would score ~0 regardless. The 0.09 result
  is **over-determined** and cannot distinguish "SF-TD collapses" from "the readout cannot
  express goal-reaching."

### Empirical signature (wandb) confirms "well-fit critic, wrong objective" — not collapse
| Cell | success (3 seeds) | actor q_mean | sf_loss | phi_absmax |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| IdentityB (agnostic) | .072–.112 | 24.1 | **4.7 (converged)** | 28.9 (healthy) |
| Frozen50 (agnostic) | .060–.116 | 26.6 | 5.9 | 28.7 |
| IdentityCond / FrozenCond | .096–.180 | **561–690 (25× inflated)** | 4.5–9.8 | 437–605 |
| *-Ortho1k (all 4) | ~0 | ~0 (Q crushed) | 1.6–2.2 | 5–11 |
| *-DDPGBC (4 cells) | .008–.156 | same scales as flow | | |

The agnostic cells solved their vector TD (loss ~5, sane scales) and still scored 0.09 —
**no optimization collapse occurred**. Ortho1k on φ directly fights the TD fit (drives Q→0)
— a knowably dead knob, not evidence. The Cond cells add a 25× scale inflation. The
flow-vs-DDPGBC control compares two actors on a wrong-reward critic — uninformative.

### Secondary code issues (real but dominated by the confound)
- **Goal-dependent masks in a goal-agnostic target**: `datasets.py:242` makes `masks`
  depend on the relabeled goal; in agnostic cells the target `b + γ·mask·φ'` varies with
  g_i while φ(s,a) cannot — φ fits a mush over the goal mix.
- Readout takes `min(q1,q2)` over two independently-TD'd SF heads; ref uses min-L2-norm
  target selection (known fidelity gap #2). Second-order.
- The 11 green tests verify *mechanics* (γ=0 semantics, frozen ψ, conditioning), not
  *task semantics* — they could not have caught this.

### Corrected reading
What the campaign actually supports: **a sparse goal indicator is not linearly expressible
in identity/frozen-random features** — you need a basis learned to make rewards linearly
readable, which is exactly the job FB's learned-B + ortho is attempting (and struggling
with) on manipulation. This *strengthens* the readout-side half of the bridge thesis while
retracting the training-collapse half.

## 3. Measure-only / measure-graft code: faithful, with three caveats

Line-by-line check of `measure_loss` against canonical FB (Touati & Ollivier):
- Diagonal written as `−mean(diag(M − γ·sg[M']))` vs FB's `−mean(diag(M))`: target is
  stop-gradded ⇒ extra term is a constant ⇒ **gradient-equivalent**. Fine.
- Ortho = ½-scaled sample estimator of ‖E[ψψᵀ]−I‖²: absorbed into λ. Fine.
- Target-net usage, per-row z consistency (z_i = sg ψ(vgoals_i), a⁺ = π(s'_i, vgoals_i)),
  einsum shapes: correct.

Caveats that ride on every measure-cell number:
1. **Uncontrolled scale regime** (§1): actor q_mean 2.5k–4.8k, grads 12–24k, occasional
   seed crashes. Numbers are "what this implementation does," but it operates at the edge
   of blowup; FB-native scale discipline (norm caps) is absent.
2. **z-distribution deviation:** z is always ψ(GCIQL's goal mix); canonical FB draws ~50%
   random directions z~N(0,I) sphere-projected. Never tested whether this matters.
3. **No termination masking in the measure TD** (FB-faithful, infinite-horizon occupancy)
   while IQL bootstraps through absorbing goals — the two objectives fit different
   quantities at the goal. Bake one sentence into the paper.

**New mechanistic datum from the pull** (free figure for the paper): measure off-diagonal
TD residual = **6347** for z-blind φ(s,a) (MeasureOnly — it never fits its own objective)
vs **266** with `fb_forward_z` (FBz — fits the density 24× better) — yet FBz still only
scores 25%. The density is unfittable without a task index, *and fitting it well is still
insufficient for control* — direct support for the supervise-what-you-consume / projection-
mismatch mechanism.

## 4. Exp4 `actor_z_infer` ≈ 0 is an artifact — inference-consistent conditioning is untested

`_infer_actor_z` (`gciql_flow.py` ~line 321) builds z as `(uniform<0.1 mask + eye) @ ψ`:
at batch 1024 that sums ~102 *random* goal embeddings against 1 true one — the "inferred z"
is ~99% noise by construction. It models a dense random reward, not the goal indicator the
G4 eval actually infers with (`z = E[r·ψ]`, r peaked at the goal). The runs also show the
actor seeing a detached Q scale (−1.76 vs critic −19.2). Wandb: s0 0.0, s1 0.008, s2
crashed @95k.

**Therefore the fix candidate for the G4 dissociation (exact z=ψ(g) → 0.93 vs inferred
z=E[rψ] → 0.07, same critic, cf-AUC 0.92) has never been properly tried.** A correct
version weights ψ_j by the actual relabeled indicator/soft kernel around g_i (≈ retrieval
of the goal's embedding + near-neighbors), matching the test-time inference rule.
