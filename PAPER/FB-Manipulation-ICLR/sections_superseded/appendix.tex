\section{Additional experimental detail}

\subsection{Divergence: the full control set}
\label{app:diverge}

Section~\ref{sec:exp-diverge} reports the frozen-embedding experiment in
condensed form. The full set of controls:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Frozen $\psi$, density estimator.} Critic scale
$154 \to 5.4\times10^{4}$ within 65k steps; success decays
$0.98\to0.60\to0.42$ by 100k (3 seeds). The embedding is taken from a checkpoint
that supports $0.97$ control and is held fixed, so representation quality cannot
account for the outcome.
\item \textbf{Trainable $\psi$, density estimator.} Collapses faster:
$0.97\to0.07$ and $0.98\to0.06$ by 50k (2 seeds). Representation corruption
compounds the estimator defect but is not its cause.
\item \textbf{Frozen $\psi$, bounded region estimator.} Scale plateaus on the
Monte-Carlo estimate of the true discounted visitation mass; both seeds complete
500k steps without instability.
\item \textbf{Orthonormality retained.} Scale is bounded, but control is
suppressed to $0.225$ --- far below the $0.93$ goal-conditioned bar. This is the
other half of the trade described in Section~\ref{sec:singular}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{A tabular instance of the same structure}
\label{app:playpen}

The structural claims of Section~\ref{sec:method} can be exhibited exactly in a
$50$-state MDP built with the same anatomy as manipulation play data: a $7\times7$
grid plus a pit, in which the \emph{column} is the semantic coordinate (the
region variable) and the \emph{row} is an interaction mode invisible to the
region. A $2\times2$ sticky bowl models a cube at rest; the top row is a conveyor
that drifts two columns per step regardless of action, modeling a carried cube
whose position cannot be held; riding off its end drops into a hard-to-leave pit.

Fitting rank-$8$ bilinear $F^{\top}B$ on $20$k offline transitions with identical
architecture and data, the region regression reaches relative error
$0.05$--$0.09$ and per-state goal-ranking correlation $0.96$--$0.99$, while the
density-TD form reaches only $0.38$/$0.65$; adding the orthonormality regularizer
makes the latter \emph{worse} ($0.53$/$0.45$). Two honest notes. With no actor in
the loop the density estimator fits poorly but does not diverge, which is
consistent with the divergence of Section~\ref{sec:exp-diverge} being a property
of the estimator-plus-actor system rather than of the loss alone. And
absorbing-first and direct visitation regression perform alike here
($0.09$ vs $0.05$), consistent with the curriculum's value lying in the control
loop rather than in policy evaluation.

This environment also exhibits the ranking disagreement that motivates the
visitation backup: weighting a stable region and a transient one so that they are
nearly equally \emph{reachable}, ranking by reaching value selects the transient
region while ranking by occupancy selects the stable one, and exact policy
evaluation confirms the latter ($96.8$ vs $47.9$ discounted return).

\subsection{Goal-decoding extraction routes}
\label{app:decode}

Earlier versions of this work evaluated a different reward suite through
goal-\emph{decoding} routes: inferring $z_r$, decoding it to a single dataset
state by top-$k$ reward-weighted retrieval, and conditioning the actor on that
state. Those routes reach $0.73$--$0.99$ on every family whose optimum reduces to
one dataset state, and fail on families that do not. Because the outcome is
determined by the decode rather than by the measure, we do not use them in the
main text; the $48$-reward suite of Section~\ref{sec:exp-l2} consumes $z_r$
directly.

\subsection{Reward construction}
\label{app:rewards}

All transfer rewards are functions of the cube position $\cube(x)$, with centers
drawn uniformly from dataset states. Per family, $8$ instances:
\textsc{ball}~$\epsilon$ is $\ind{\|\cube(x)-c_0\|\le\epsilon}$ for
$\epsilon\in\{0.4,0.8,1.6\}$ in observation units where the trained kernel is
$0.4$; \textsc{smooth}~$\sigma$ is $\exp(-\|\cube(x)-c_0\|^2/2\sigma^2)$ for
$\sigma\in\{0.4,0.8\}$; \textsc{twozone} is
$\max_k\ind{\|\cube(x)-c_k\|\le 0.4}$ with $\|c_1-c_2\|>1.6$. Inference uses
$m{=}10{,}000$ relabeled rows. Stage A scores prediction from $12$ teleported
starts $\times$ $2$ rollouts; stage B scores control over $10$ episodes per drive
at horizon $200$.

The synthetic rewards used to populate the task-vector manifold in
Section~\ref{sec:exp-rewardz} are drawn per batch element: \textsc{kernel} mixes
balls, Gaussians and two-zone rewards with centers drawn from the batch and
log-uniform radii in $[0.2,2.0]$; \textsc{rff} draws
$r=\mathrm{relu}(\cos(w^{\top}\cube+\varphi))$ with $w\sim\mathcal{N}(0,I)/b$ and
$\varphi\sim\mathrm{U}[0,2\pi)$. Both are normalized before the moment readout;
the projection to the task-vector sphere makes their scale immaterial.
