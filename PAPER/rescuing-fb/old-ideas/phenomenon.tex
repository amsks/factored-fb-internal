\section{Setup and notation}\label{sec:setup}

MDP $(\mathcal S,\mathcal A,P,\gamma)$, $\gamma=0.99$; offline dataset $\mathcal D$ of play
episodes from a scripted pick-and-place policy $\mu$ (OGBench \texttt{cube-single-play}, 1M
transitions); $\rho$ the dataset state marginal. The successor measure of a policy $\pi$,
\begin{equation}
M^\pi(s,a,X)\;=\;\sum_{t\ge 0}\gamma^t\,\Pr\!\big[s_{t+1}\in X\mid s_0{=}s,a_0{=}a,\pi\big],
\qquad X\subseteq\mathcal S,
\label{eq:sm}
\end{equation}
is the object FB factorizes, $M^{\pi_z}(s,a,\mathrm ds')\approx F(s,a,z)^\top B(s')\rho(\mathrm
ds')$, with the reward readout $Q_r(s,a)=F(s,a,z_r)^\top z_r$, $z_r=\E_\rho[r(s')B(s')]$.
GC-IQL trains the same normalized bilinear head $Q(s,a,g)=\phi(s,a)^\top\psi(g)/\sqrt d$ by
hindsight-relabelled sparse-reward expectile TD ($r\in\{-1,0\}$, mask $0$ at the goal). Both
sides drive the identical FQL-style flow actor. Success numbers are OGBench
\texttt{cube-single} 5-task means; anchors: bilinear GC-IQL \numgood{0.904}, plain FB
\numbad{0.42} @1M, published GC-IQL 0.68.

\section{The phenomenon, stated}\label{sec:statement}

\begin{claim}[The phenomenon]\label{claim:main}
The successor-measure TD objective \emph{is} scalar TD --- performed simultaneously on the
family of Dirac rewards --- but with three estimator choices that, on play-distributed
manipulation data, jointly destroy the action-ordering information the task requires:
\textbf{(A)} it weights test tasks by state occupancy $\rho$ rather than by the task
distribution; \textbf{(B)} it bootstraps through a learned policy (non-contractive, and its
only improvement mechanism) or, if made contractive (SARSA), loses improvement entirely;
\textbf{(C)} it fits \emph{revisit-counting occupancancy} semantics rather than
\emph{first-passage} semantics, and under a re-grasping play policy the occupancy of any test
state is mixing-dominated: $82\%$ of its $\gamma$-mass lies at horizons where futures are
action-independent. Consequently the objective's sensitivity to the action taken now --- the
only thing the actor consumes --- is orders of magnitude below its sensitivity to
arm-configuration occupancy structure, its fixed point carries a near-vacuous advantage
ordering, and \emph{no conditioning, centering, scaling, conservatism, or bootstrap repair
inside the occupancy corner can fix it}, because each such repair changes the optimization
path toward the same fixed point. The bilinear structure, the $z$-space, and the moment
inference API are all healthy; only the grounding is wrong. Replacing the grounding by
task-weighted, in-sample, first-passage scalar TD --- keeping every other FB component ---
restores control ($0.825$ zero-shot, inference gap $2.7$ points).
\end{claim}

The rest of this note makes each clause precise, checks it against all 12 constraint facts
plus the newly landed T7/T8, and then derives the modifications that follow.

\section{The bridge identity: measure TD is scalar TD on Dirac tasks}\label{sec:identity}

\begin{proposition}[Exact rewriting of the FB loss]\label{prop:identity}
Let $m_{s^+}(s,a):=F(s,a,z)^\top B(s^+)$ denote the factorized measure density at test point
$s^+$, and consider the implicit Dirac reward $r_{s^+}(s')=\delta_{s^+}(s')/\rho(s^+)$. Then
the FB measure-TD loss with batch test points $s^+\sim\rho$,
\begin{equation}
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{FB}}
=\E_{(s,a,s')\sim\mathcal D,\;s^+\sim\rho}\Big[\big(m_{s^+}(s,a)-\gamma\,\sg\,\bar
m_{s^+}(s',a')\big)^2\Big]
-2\,\E_{(s,a,s')}\big[m_{s'}(s,a)\big],\qquad a'\sim\pi_z(s'),
\label{eq:fbloss}
\end{equation}
equals, up to an additive constant independent of the parameters, the $\rho$-weighted
family of scalar TD losses
\begin{equation}
\E_{s^+\sim\rho}\;\E_{(s,a,s')}\Big[\big(m_{s^+}(s,a)-r_{s^+}(s')-\gamma\,\sg\,\bar
m_{s^+}(s',a')\big)^2\Big].
\label{eq:scalarfamily}
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Expand the square in \eqref{eq:scalarfamily}: the cross term
$-2\,\E_{s^+\sim\rho}\E[(m_{s^+}(s,a)-\gamma\bar m_{s^+})\,r_{s^+}(s')]$ collapses under
$\E_{s^+\sim\rho}[\,f(s^+)\delta_{s^+}(s')/\rho(s^+)\,]=f(s')$ to
$-2\,\E[(m_{s'}(s,a)-\gamma\bar m_{s'})]$; the $\gamma\bar m$ part of it is a
parameter-dependent term FB drops (target networks make it stop-gradient in practice), and
$\E[r_{s^+}^2]$ is constant. What remains is exactly \eqref{eq:fbloss}.
\end{proof}

So FB and GC-IQL train \emph{the same bilinear object by the same kind of regression}, and
differ only in four estimator coordinates:

\begin{tabular}{@{}l l l l@{}}
\toprule

\textbf{A} task weighting & test tasks $\sim\rho$ (occupancy) & hindsight mix

\end{center}

\begin{corollary}\label{cor:corner}
GC-IQL \emph{is} FB after moving all four axes. ``FB fails, GC-IQL works'' is therefore not a
comparison of two methods but the statement that some subset of $\{A,B,C\}$ is fatal ($D$ is
a $6$-point detail). Every failed measure cell in the program --- plain FB $0.42$, measure-only
$0.08$--$0.34$, C4 rebuild $0.165$, T10-b2 $0.06$--$0.10$, T7 $0.23$--$0.24$, T8 $0.067$ ---
shares \emph{all three} of A, B, C at the FB corner. No cell has yet moved exactly one of
A, B, C; \S\ref{sec:direction2} defines those cells.
\end{corollary}

\section{The quantitative core: temporal misalignment}\label{sec:misalignment}

The folk explanation --- ``play data is arm-dominated, so occupancies fit arm features'' ---
is \textbf{false at the level of raw mass} and must not be printed: the cube is \emph{held on
45.5\% of dataset steps}, cube dimensions carry 26\% of the observation's marginal variance,
and per-step normalized motion share does not separate cube from antmaze in the needed
direction (all measured model-free from the raw \texttt{npz}; misalignment note 2026-07-13).
What is true is sharper and temporal.

Let $G_t\in\{\text{held},\text{free}\}$ be the (observed, \texttt{obs[18]}) interaction gate
and $c_t$ the cube position. Measured on the raw dataset:
\begin{equation}
R_K:=\frac{\E\big[\|c_{t+K}-c_t\|\;\big|\;G_t=\text{held}\big]}
{\E\big[\|c_{t+K}-c_t\|\;\big|\;G_t=\text{free}\big]}
=\begin{cases}
130\text{--}150\times & K\le 2\\
55\times & K=5\\
7.8\times & K=10\\
1.0\times & K\ge 50,
\end{cases}
\label{eq:gate}
\end{equation}
because the scripted play policy re-grasps: long-horizon occupancy \emph{forgets the gate}.
Against this, the discount kernel $\propto\gamma^t$ of \eqref{eq:sm} places $82\%$ of its
mass at $K>20$.

\begin{proposition}[Misalignment bound]\label{prop:misalignment}
Suppose the behavior-policy $K$-step kernels mix over actions:
$\sup_{a_1,a_2}\TV{P^\mu_K(\cdot\mid s,a_1)-P^\mu_K(\cdot\mid s,a_2)}\le\varepsilon_K$ with
$\varepsilon_K$ decaying as in \eqref{eq:gate}. Then for any test function $f$ with
$\|f\|_\infty\le 1$ (any bounded reward or backward feature),
\begin{equation}
\big|\,\langle M^{\mu}(s,a_1,\cdot)-M^{\mu}(s,a_2,\cdot),f\rangle\,\big|
\;\le\;\sum_{t\le K_0}\gamma^{t}\,\varepsilon_t\;+\;\frac{\gamma^{K_0}}{1-\gamma}\,
\varepsilon_{K_0},
\label{eq:bound}
\end{equation}
i.e.\ the action-dependence of the occupancy object is confined to the pre-mixing window.
With the measured profile ($K_0\approx 20$--$50$), the second term --- $82\%$ of the total
discounted mass --- contributes essentially nothing: \emph{the occupancy functional of the
data policy genuinely does not require the interaction variable at the horizons where its
mass lives}. Control, by contrast, is decided at $K\le 5$, where the future is
$55$--$150\times$ mode-determined.
\end{proposition}

Three corollaries, each independently verified:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=2pt]
\item \emph{Fitting the measure well is insufficient.} The $z$-conditioned graft cut the
measure's own TD residual $24\times$ (6347$\to$266) and moved success only to $0.255$ (C7).
The bound says why: the residual is dominated by the mixing-window mass, which is
advantage-free; you can fit $99\%$ of the objective while resolving none of the ordering.
\item \emph{The domain flip.} On antmaze the short- and long-horizon conditional structure
are the same smooth diffusion ($R_K\le 2$ at every $K$ for every observable split tried) ---
there is no misalignment to pay, and measure-only scores $0.79$ vs the $0.837$ anchor there,
against $0.10$ vs $0.90$ on cube (mdose).
\item \emph{Warm starts cannot help.} The misalignment is a property of the \emph{fixed
point}, not the path: initialize at the converged $0.93$ scalar-TD solution and fade
$\mathcal L_{\mathrm{IQL}}$ out $\Rightarrow$ success collapses to $0.05$ in lockstep with
the fade (iql-fade). Centering + scale anchors + split bootstrap (T10-b2) raised the early
transient $3\times$ (0.18--0.26 @50k) and then decayed to the same $0.06$--$0.10$ ceiling:
\emph{path repaired, fixed point unchanged} --- exactly what
Claim~\ref{claim:main} predicts for any within-corner repair.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{remark}[First-passage vs.\ occupancy, sharpened]\label{rem:firstpassage}
Under first-passage semantics with absorption at the goal, the value is an affine function of
$\E[\gamma^{T_{\mathrm{hit}}}]$: dropping the cube delays $T_{\mathrm{hit}}$ by the
re-acquisition time $\tau$ and multiplies the value by $\gamma^{\tau}$ --- a persistent,
non-washing penalty ($\gamma^{30}\approx0.74$ per drop). Under occupancy semantics the same
drop merely \emph{shifts revisit mass later}; because the re-grasping data policy recovers
the cube, the discounted deficit is second-order and the post-drop state is scored nearly as
well as the held state. The $0/{-1}$-with-mask convention GC-IQL trains \emph{is}
first-passage; this semantics was never transplanted onto the measure --- it is the single
cheapest untested axis move (\S\ref{sec:direction2}).
\end{remark}

\begin{proposition}[Unsupported boundary]\label{prop:support}
Of $12{,}144$ grasp releases in the 1M-transition dataset, $12{,}127$ are deliberate places
and $17$ are mid-air drops ($\rho$-mass $1.7\times10^{-5}$). For any objective that is an
expectation under $\rho$ (every objective in this program), relabelling those 17 transitions
arbitrarily changes the loss by $O(10^{-5})\cdot L$: the stable-vs-slipping value cliff ---
precisely the evaluation failure mode --- is statistically invisible, and no bounded
reweighting can amplify what is absent. Consequence: nothing may be asked to \emph{rank
grasp-stability counterfactuals}; the value's job is to order \emph{in-support}
grasp-maintaining actions, and the BC-anchored (flow / selection) extraction is load-bearing
(bilinear+DDPG-BC $0.51$ $<$ bilinear+flow $0.90$). Any proposal that loosens the BC anchor
on cube is predicted to fail regardless of the critic.
\end{proposition}

\section{Axis B, resolved by T7 and T8 (new, 2026-07-20)}\label{sec:axisB}

The two arms that landed today close axis B's two open corners.

\paragraph{T8: contraction without improvement is worthless.} One-step FB (SARSA
dataset-action bootstrap, a $\gamma$-contraction) with $F$ kept $z$-conditioned scores
$0.02/0.06/0.12\to\mathbf{0.067}$, statistically indistinguishable from its $z$-zeroed
matched control ($0.058$, 10 seeds) --- and from the BC-flow floor ($\approx0.06$). This is
the clean reading of the phenomenon: a contractive backup \emph{estimates} $M^\mu$
faithfully, but $M^\mu$'s advantage ordering is exactly the object
Proposition~\ref{prop:misalignment} bounds --- greedifying the behavior measure's readout
adds \emph{nothing} over BC on this data. One-step FB fails under both readings of the
$z$-conditioning dispute; the ablation was worth running precisely because it kills both.

\paragraph{T7: the learned-policy bootstrap's optimism is not the bug --- it is the only
thing FB has.} Exact min-aggregation (pessimism $0.5$; verified $\equiv\min$ for twin heads)
drops plain FB $0.42\to\mathbf{0.240}$; beyond-min ($1.0$) $\to0.227$. Conservatism
\emph{hurts}, dose-independently. Interpretation inside the phenomenon: plain FB's $0.42$
$>$ one-step's $0.067$ because the $a'\sim\pi_z$ bootstrap is FB's sole improvement
mechanism --- it is what lifts FB off the BC floor at all --- and pessimism shrinks the
already-marginal advantage signal toward the ensemble's common (baseline) component. FB's
pathology was never optimistic over-estimation of off-support actions (that is the DDPG+BC
pathology of the \emph{bilinear scalar} critic); it is an \emph{uninformative ordering}, and
you cannot fix a wrong ordering by shrinking it. This closes the VC-FB confound: the failure
claim needs no conservative-FB restatement.

\begin{corollary}[Axis B is a dilemma, not a knob]\label{cor:dilemma}
Within the measure corner, the bootstrap choice is: learned-$\pi$ (improvement, no
contraction, ceiling $0.42$), SARSA (contraction, no improvement, floor $0.067$), or the
untested middle --- an \emph{in-sample expectile improvement on the measure}
(\S\ref{sec:direction2}, the exact transformation that took scalar GC-Bellman from unstable
to $0.904$). That cell is the only remaining way axis B could be exonerated or convicted.
\end{corollary}

\section{Scale identifiability (why the runaways were predictable)}\label{sec:scale}

The scalar objective's units are pinned by the reward: $|Q|\le(1-\gamma)^{-1}$. The measure
objective's units are density units $1/\rho$, and the factorization $F^\top B$ splits them
non-identifiably ($F\to cF$, $B\to B/c$). Every observed runaway is this one defect: T10-b
v1 (both legs scale-normalized $\Rightarrow$ objective scale-invariant $\Rightarrow$ diagonal
attraction inflates $q$ to $10^{13}$), SG-ISFB's unbounded $\langle F,z\rangle$ drift, the
$\psi(s,g)$ leg's norm runaway under scalar TD (T2: $\E\|\psi\|^2$ monotone in divergence).
\textbf{Design rule (binding):} every future objective must answer ``what pins the absolute
scale?'' before launch --- computable rewards with units, a masked/bounded fixed point, or a
hard norm anchor on exactly one leg.

\section{The record against the phenomenon}\label{sec:record}

Claim~\ref{claim:main} is not one more hypothesis; it is the unique statement consistent with
all of: C1--C12 (constraint set, Fable\_Suggestions \S0), the T10 closure (aux $0.823$ ---
never helps, costs 8 points; anchored-only $0.06$--$0.10$ --- path not fixed point), T7
($0.240/0.227$ --- conservatism hurts), T8 ($0.067$ --- contraction alone is the BC floor),
the misalignment measurements (\S\ref{sec:misalignment}), the domain flip (antmaze $0.79$),
the in-distribution phase evidence (FB fails at C1 $=0.43$ with grasp $=1.000$ and
$P(\mathrm{success}\mid\mathrm{secured})=0.437$ vs $0.982$ --- post-grasp ordering, not
generalization), and the healthy-API results (E1a moment inference $0.955$; RG-FB v0
$0.825\pm0.014$ with a $2.7$-point inference gap).

\begin{claim}[Licensed negative claim --- final wording]\label{claim:negative}
On play-distributed manipulation data (OGBench cube-single), \emph{no tested form of
successor-measure TD grounding supports manipulation}: the native objective, its conditioned,
normalized, centered, scale-anchored, conservative, one-step, auxiliary, curriculum, and
warm-started variants all converge to a $0.06$--$0.42$ band whose failures are post-grasp
ordering failures, while the identical architecture, actor, data, and inference API under
scalar first-passage TD reaches $0.90$ (supervised) / $0.825$ (zero-shot). The mechanism is
the temporal occupancy--task misalignment of Proposition~\ref{prop:misalignment} (with the
support boundary of Proposition~\ref{prop:support}); first-passage/hindsight-weighted
measure objects (\S\ref{sec:direction2}) are excluded from the claim's scope because they
are no longer the successor measure.
\end{claim}

\section{Direction I --- the constructive program (near-certain, run in this
order)}\label{sec:direction1}

The phenomenon dictates the repair: \emph{keep FB-the-interface (bilinear factorization,
$z$-space, moment inference, $z$-conditioned flow actor), replace FB-the-estimator with
task-weighted in-sample first-passage scalar TD, and re-introduce FB's task-distribution
training through the reward, not the measure.} Each rung below is decomposed into steps that
are either theorems or already-measured numbers; the only genuinely new risk in each is
named, with a guard.

\subsection{GPI over goal candidates at inference (zero training; theorem-backed)}
\label{sec:gpi}

T6's sole zero-shot failure is composite rewards: two-goal max-mixtures score $0.260$ while
$\cos(z,\psi(g_k))=0.77$--$0.82$ --- inference is fine; the actor has never seen an
off-Dirac $z$. Classical GPI repairs this with no gradients. For $r=\max_k r_{g_k}$ with
candidate set $Z=\{z_r,\psi(g_1),\dots,\psi(g_K)\}$:
\begin{equation}
a_j=\pi(s,z_j)\ \ \forall j,\qquad \text{execute } a_{j^*},\quad
j^*=\arg\max_j Q(s,a_j,z_r).
\label{eq:gpi}
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[GPI applies verbatim]\label{prop:gpi}
$Q(\cdot,\cdot,z_r)$ is linear in $z$, so it is evaluated exactly where the critic was
trained (a convex combination of Dirac readouts), and each candidate action is an on-support
Dirac-policy action --- the region where the critic's ordering is measured near-perfect
(rerank $0.91$--$0.96$). By the GPI theorem (Barreto et al.), the induced policy's value
under $r$ dominates every candidate's: $V^{\mathrm{GPI}}_r(s)\ge\max_j V^{\pi_{z_j}}_r(s)$
up to the critic's evaluation error on on-support actions. Since reaching \emph{either} goal
satisfies a max-mixture and per-goal success is $\approx0.9$, the prediction is
$0.260\to\gtrsim0.8$.
\end{proposition}

If GPI does \emph{not} fix mixtures, the failure is not actor coverage --- re-open the E1a
ladder before touching training. Cost: $K{+}1$ critic passes per step, CPU, hours.

\subsection{RG-FB at $d{=}512$ + selection extraction (both anchors already measured)}
\label{sec:ladder}

The v0 gap to the supervised anchor is training-side ($0.825$ vs $0.904$; inference gap
$2.7$ pts). Two rungs with measured ceilings: (i) \textbf{d512 + $z$-actor}: the G4 anchor at
$d512$ is $0.921$ --- v0 is $d50$; this is the known cheapest points. (ii) \textbf{IDQL-style
selection at deployment}: $N{\approx}32$ BC-flow proposals, argmax frozen $Q$ --- the rerank
instrument measured the bilinear critic's ordering worth $0.947$--$0.96$ \emph{with no actor
training}, and selection is immune to both known extraction failure modes (off-support peak;
anchor fights). Neither rung contains an untested mechanism.

\subsection{RG-FB v1: task-prior scalar grounding (the headline combine)}
\label{sec:v1}

FB's real asset was never the measure loss --- it is that actor and critic are trained
\emph{across a distribution over tasks}. v0 trains both only at $z=\psi(g_{\mathrm{hindsight}})$;
T6 shows the cost (mixtures $0.260$). v1 restores FB's task distribution through
\emph{computable rewards}, grounding every sampled task with the same expectile machinery:
per batch, sample
\begin{align}
\text{w.p. } p_1&:\ \text{Dirac goal (v0): } r(s')=\mathbf 1[\|s'-g\|\le\varepsilon],\ \ z=\psi(g);\nonumber\\
\text{w.p. } p_2&:\ \text{$K$-goal mixture: } r(s')=\max_k \mathbf 1[\|s'-g_k\|\le\varepsilon],\ \
z=\Sigma_k w_k\psi(g_k)\ \text{(match-data renorm)};\label{eq:taskprior}\\
\text{w.p. } p_3&:\ \text{random linear reward: } r(s')=\sg[\psi(s')]^\top\tilde z/\sqrt d,\ \
\tilde z\sim\text{sphere}\times\overline{\|\psi\|},\nonumber
\end{align}
then apply GC-IQL's expectile value regression and scalar Bellman regression \emph{at that
$z$}, with the flow actor conditioned on $\sg[z]$. Nothing else changes.

\begin{remark}[Why this is the principled combine]
By Proposition~\ref{prop:identity}, measure TD is scalar TD marginalized over Dirac tasks at
$\rho$-weighting with a learned-$\pi$ bootstrap. v1 is scalar TD marginalized over a
\emph{chosen} task prior with the in-sample bootstrap: it keeps the actual source of
zero-shot-ness (training across tasks) and replaces every diagnosed pathology --- axis A
becomes task-shaped, axis B contractive-with-improvement, the scale is pinned because every
sampled reward has units (\S\ref{sec:scale}), and the linear-in-$z$ critic structure is
\emph{supervised} at off-Dirac $z$ instead of assumed by extrapolation. The one new risk is
multi-task interference on the Dirac anchor; guard with the pre-registered non-regression
band (Dirac $\ge0.80$; two-goal max-mixture $\ge0.60$ success $/\le0.30$ dead; regions
$\ge0.672$).
\end{remark}

\subsection{The measure, kept --- as a stop-gradient readout head}
\label{sec:sghead}

Do \emph{not} re-add the measure loss at any $\lambda$ on shared parameters (T10-a: $-8$
points; E2: at best free; the metamotivo aux-$Q$ summed with the measure: $0.468$, a null at
10 seeds). But two things about the measure survive intact and should be said and used:

\begin{remark}[RG-FB does not discard the successor object --- it swaps it]
\label{rem:fpkernel}
Under the $0/{-1}$-with-mask convention, the trained value is an affine transform of the
\emph{first-passage kernel}:
\begin{equation}
\E\big[\gamma^{T_{\mathrm{hit}}(g)}\big]\;=\;1+(1-\gamma)\,Q(s,a,g),
\label{eq:fpidentity}
\end{equation}
so $\phi^\top\psi/\sqrt d$ is a rank-$d$ factorization of a bona fide successor-type object
with first-passage (control-matched) semantics --- Remark~\ref{rem:firstpassage} at the level
of the trained head. The SF-style extractables carry over: temporal distances
$\hat T(s,g)=\log_\gamma\!\big(1+(1-\gamma)Q\big)$, reachability sets, linear goal-set
composition + GPI (\S\ref{sec:gpi}), and moment task inference. What is genuinely
occupancy-specific --- density queries, $\gamma$-model rollouts, OPE of dense rewards ---
is recovered below.
\end{remark}

\textbf{The sg readout head.} Fit a full measure factorization \emph{on top of} the frozen
control towers,
\begin{equation}
\widehat M(s,a,s^+)\;=\;\phi_M\big(\sg[\phi(s,a)]\big)^{\top}\,
\psi_M\big(\sg[\psi(s^+)]\big)/\sqrt{d_M},
\label{eq:sghead}
\end{equation}
trained by the measure objective (or by direct Monte-Carlo occupancy regression) with
\emph{no gradient path into $\phi,\psi$}. Every measured cost of ``measure $+$ scalar''
arrived through shared parameters (E2 stop-grad localization; T10-a); cutting the sharing
removes the conflict \emph{by construction}, at zero control risk. This yields the honest
measure fidelity number for the paper --- evaluated against empirical discounted occupancy
on held-out trajectories, broken down by horizon bucket ($K{\le}5$ / $5$--$20$ / ${>}20$, so
the misalignment bound of Prop.~\ref{prop:misalignment} is visible in the calibration) ---
plus working density/OPE extractables. The pre-registered comparison: if the head on
scalar-trained features is at least as occupancy-faithful as plain FB's native $F^\top B$,
the sentence is ``scalar grounding is measure-\emph{free} but not measure-\emph{blind}'';
if it is worse, print the trade --- either way the control result is untouched.
Implementation: spec 06, item 8.

\section{Direction II --- single-axis measure cells (mechanism work, optional for the
claim)}\label{sec:direction2}

With T7/T8/T10 dead, these cells no longer gate the negative claim
(Claim~\ref{claim:negative} survives them semantically: a first-passage or
hindsight-weighted object is not \emph{the} successor measure). Their value is converting
``all tested forms fail'' into ``axis X is the toxic one.'' Each is a small delta to the
existing loss; prior on any single axis rescuing FB is low; run cheapest-diagnosis first.

\subsection{Axis C: first-passage FB (one masking line)}
Replace occupancy semantics with first-occupancy semantics
($F(s,a,s^+)=\E[\gamma^{T_{\mathrm{hit}}(s^+)}]$): with batch test points, ``the test point
is hit'' is a tolerance ball around the diagonal, $h_{ij}=\mathbf 1[\|s'_i-s^+_j\|\le
\varepsilon]$ ($\varepsilon=$ the 4cm success radius on cube-xyz coordinates):
\begin{equation}
\delta_{ij}=M_{ij}-h_{ij}-\gamma\,(1-h_{ij})\,\sg[\bar M_{ij}].
\label{eq:firstpassage}
\end{equation}
Same fixed-point semantics as GC-IQL's masked sparse-reward value, per column; bilinear
factorization, $z$-space, inference untouched; also removes the worst non-contraction
amplifier (unbounded revisit mass). \emph{Read:} $\gg0.10$ $\Rightarrow$ semantics was a/the
toxic axis (Remark~\ref{rem:firstpassage} validated at the loss level); $\approx0.10$
$\Rightarrow$ reframe the $82\%$ number as correlate, not cause.

\subsection{Axis B: expectile measure backup (the untested middle of
Corollary~\ref{cor:dilemma})}
Each test-point column of $M$ is a scalar value function, so IQL applies pointwise:
introduce $V_M(s,s^+)=\phi_V(s)^\top\tilde\psi(s^+)/\sqrt d$, train by expectile regression
onto $\sg[M(s,a_{\mathrm{data}},s^+)]$, and back up
\begin{equation}
M(s,a,s^+)\ \leftarrow\ r_\delta+\gamma\,\sg[V_M(s',s^+)]
\qquad\text{(no $a'$ of any kind)}.
\label{eq:expectilemeasure}
\end{equation}
Contraction restored \emph{and} improvement retained --- the exact transformation that fixed
the scalar side. Composes with \eqref{eq:firstpassage}; if singles are both partial, run
C{+}B jointly as the ``GC-IQL-semantics measure.'' \emph{Read:} T8 $\approx0.058$ but this
lifts $\Rightarrow$ ``one-step evaluation is not enough; in-sample improvement is the missing
bootstrap.'' Floors at $\sim0.1$ $\Rightarrow$ axis B fully exonerated; A/C carry the blame.

\subsection{Axis A: hindsight-weighted test points}
Replace $s^+\sim\rho$ with the scalar side's goal mix (50\% geometric-future
same-trajectory / 30\% random / 20\% current), moving the diagonal positive term to ``a
future state of this trajectory.'' This is the loss-level test of the weighting half of
Proposition~\ref{prop:misalignment}. (Caveat to print: at this corner the object is no
longer the $\rho$-referenced measure without an importance correction $\rho_{\mathrm{future}}/\rho$
--- accept and say so.)

\subsection{Do not resurrect (all refuted)}
Centering/dueling splits of $M$ (T10-b/b2); scale-normalizing both legs (runaway); measure as
auxiliary at any $\lambda$ \emph{on shared parameters} (T10-a, E2; the sg head of
\S\ref{sec:sghead} is the permitted form); the metamotivo aux-$Q$ (\texttt{q\_loss\_coef})
summed with the measure ($0.468$ @1M, 10 seeds $\approx$ plain FB --- it moves no estimator
axis: self-referential implicit reward, learned-$\pi$ bootstrap, no mask);
curricula/annealing into a measure endpoint, \emph{from any initialization including RG-FB}
(iql-fade $5.3$; shared-legs ramp $0.9$; sg-$\phi$ ramp $84.9$ = neutralized-not-helpful:
the fixed point owns the endpoint, warm starts only change the path); $z$-zeroed one-step
($0.058$); \textbf{conservatism/min-aggregation at any dose (T7, new)}; eval-time goal
smoothing (T1); unbounded $\psi(s,g)$ heads (T2); task-norm reweighting (N1/N2); zHER /
decouple-actor-$z$; whitened inference ($\kappa\approx10^{14}$).
