\section{Shared experimental substrate}\label{sec:substrate}

\textbf{Environment and data.} OGBench \texttt{cube-single-play-v0}: a 6-DoF arm must move a
cube to a target position; observations are 28-D state vectors, actions 5-D in $[-1,1]$. The
offline dataset is 1M transitions of scripted ``play'' (repeated pick-and-place at random
locations). Evaluation: the 5 \texttt{singletask} goals, horizon 200, success = cube within
4\,cm of target (the environment's own criterion); we report mean success over
50 episodes/task (jax cells) or 10 episodes/task (torch cells).

\textbf{Torch stack} (\texttt{agents/fb/}): a faithful port of the td-jepa/metamotivo FB
implementation. $F$: two 512-unit input embeddings ($[L(s),z]$ and $[L(s),a]$) joined by a
$2{\times}512$ trunk, twin heads; $B$: 4-layer 512 MLP with output hard-normalized to
$\|B\|=\sqrt d$; a learned ``left encoder'' $L(s)$ (also $\sqrt d$-normalized) feeds $F$.
Adam, $\mathrm{lr}=10^{-4}$ ($F,B$, actor; $3{\times}10^{-4}$ for the BC vector field), batch
256, target EMA $\tau=0.005$, 1M steps. Actor = FlowBC (\S\ref{sec:flowbc}).

\textbf{Jax stack} (\texttt{tools/wandb\_mode\_shim/gciql\_flow.py}, built on the OGBench
reference GCIQL): value networks are 3-layer 512 MLPs with GELU and LayerNorm; twin critics
(\texttt{nn.vmap} ensemble), single $V$; Adam $3{\times}10^{-4}$, batch 256, $\tau=0.005$
(critic only), 500k steps, eval every 50k. Goal relabeling for the value loss: 20\% current
state, 50\% geometric future ($p=1-\gamma$), 30\% random; actor goals: uniform future on the
same trajectory. Rewards $r\in\{-1,0\}$, masks 0 at the goal.

\subsection{The FlowBC / FQL actor (used by both stacks)}\label{sec:flowbc}
Three components. (1) A \emph{behavior flow}: a vector field $v(s,x_t,t)$ (4-layer 512,
GELU) trained by conditional flow matching on dataset actions, $x_t=(1{-}t)x_0+ta$,
target velocity $a-x_0$. It is \textbf{goal-agnostic} in the default configuration. (2) A
\emph{one-shot actor} $\pi(s,c,\varepsilon)$ (2-layer 512, tanh output) mapping Gaussian
noise to an action, conditioned on $c$ = the goal $g$, or the task vector $z$. (3) The
actor loss couples a $Q$ term with a distillation anchor:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal L_\pi = -\E\big[\mathrm{agg}_i\, Q_i(s,\pi(s,c,\varepsilon),\cdot)\big]/\sg|Q|
\;+\; \beta\,\big\|\pi(s,c,\varepsilon)-\mathrm{Euler}_{10}(v;s,\varepsilon)\big\|^2,
\qquad \beta=3.0,
\end{equation}
where $\mathrm{agg}$ = min of twin heads (jax) or mean (torch). At evaluation only the
one-shot actor is called (fresh noise each step). The distillation anchor is what makes this
family ``anchored extraction'': the policy searches near the behavior manifold and consumes
the critic only as an on-support ranking.

\section{The FB family, variant by variant}\label{sec:variants}

\subsection{Plain FB (the baseline)}\label{sec:plainfb}
FB \emph{[Touati \& Ollivier 2021]} factorizes the discounted state-occupancy (successor)
measure of a family of policies: $M^{\pi_z}(s,a,\cdot)\approx F(s,z,a)^\top B(\cdot)\rho(\cdot)$
with $Q(s,a,z)=F(s,z,a)^\top z$ and $\pi_z=\arg\max_a Q$. Training samples a minibatch,
treats its next-states $\{s'_j\}$ as measure test points, and minimizes
\begin{equation}
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{FB}}
= \frac{1}{2(B^2{-}B)}\sum_{i\neq j}\Delta_{ij}^2 - \frac1B\sum_i \Delta_{ii}
+ \lambda_\perp\,\mathcal L_\perp(B),\qquad
\Delta_{ij}=F_i^\top B(s'_j)-\gamma\,\sg[\bar F_{i'}^\top\bar B(s'_j)],
\label{eq:fbloss}
\end{equation}
with $\bar F_{i'}=\bar F(s'_i,z,a')$, $a'\sim\pi_z(s'_i)$, and
$\mathcal L_\perp = \frac{1}{2(B^2-B)}\sum_{i\ne j}(B_i^\top B_j)^2-\overline{B_i^\top B_i}$
(the diagonal term is inert here because $B$ is hard-normalized; effective
$\lambda_\perp{=}1000$ in our launches). Task vectors during training: 50\% uniform on the
$\sqrt d$ sphere, 50\% $\mathrm{proj}_{\sqrt d}B(s')$ at permuted batch next-states.
Test-time task inference: $z=\mathrm{proj}_{\sqrt d}\sum_i r_i B(s'_i)$ over 10k relabeled
transitions. \textbf{Result: 0.42 (1M) / 0.34 (500k)} vs GCIQL-MLP 0.72.
Audited deviations from canon in our port: twin-target aggregation by \emph{mean} rather than
min (an optimism delta; the conservative-FB literature identifies exactly this as harmful on
narrow data); the ortho diagonal inert; the left encoder $L(s)$.

\subsection{FB-IQL (\texttt{critic\_mode=iql})}
Replace \eqref{eq:fbloss} with the GC-IQL expectile losses applied to the FB critic
$Q(s,a,z)=F(s,z,a)^\top z$ with $z=B(g)$ and hindsight-relabeled rewards; everything else
(networks, actor, inference) unchanged. Variants differ in the reward: privileged cube-xyz
threshold, or \texttt{goal\_match} with the OGBench goal mixes; on-trajectory goal sampling
(\texttt{iql\_ontraj}) is the strongest. \textbf{Results: 0.47--0.65} --- the largest
FB-side family, still short of bilinear GCIQL's 0.904, because the FB architecture keeps two
handicaps under this objective (the $z$-conditioned $F$, worth $-6$ points in the clean graft
comparison, and the learned $B$ bottleneck, next).

\subsection{Identity-$B$ ($z{=}g$) and privileged $B$}
\emph{Identity-$B$}: delete the learned task encoder; $z=g$ directly (norms unconstrained),
$Q=F(s,z{=}g,a)^\top g$. Under IQL grounding: \textbf{0.51} vs the matched learned-$B$ control
\textbf{0.20} --- the learned $B$ is FB's single largest bottleneck. Under the measure
objective with $\sqrt d$ normalization: 0.65 (best measure-side cell, unstable).
\emph{Privileged $B$} (\texttt{fixed\_b=cube\_xyz}): $B(s)=$ the cube's xyz, $d{=}3$
(a USFA-style hand-designed feature); \textbf{0.086} --- privileged features are not the
answer; the task encoder must be co-trained with the value.

\subsection{Goal-conditioned $F$, $z$-HER, decoupled actor-$z$}
\emph{goal\_cond}: concatenate the raw goal into $F$ and the actor ($F(s,g,z,a)$): 0.25.
\emph{$z$-HER} (\texttt{train\_goal\_mode=traj}): replace the permuted-buffer $z$'s with
on-trajectory futures, $z=\mathrm{proj}B(s_{t+k})$, $k\sim\mathrm{Geom}(1-\gamma)$ truncated
at episode end: 0.43 ($=$ plain). \emph{Decoupled actor-$z$}: critic keeps the standard
$z$-mix; the actor trains only on trajectory-future $z$'s: 0.44 ($=$ plain). None of the
task-distribution levers move the needle: \textbf{the measure loss, not the $z$
distribution, is binding}.

\subsection{One-step FB (repo version) --- and the untested faithful version}
Our \texttt{onestep} flag zeroes $F$'s $z$ input and bootstraps with the dataset's next
action (SARSA): \textbf{0.06--0.08}. \emph{Important caveat}: this is \textbf{not} the
one-step FB of Zheng, Jayanth \& Eysenbach (2026), which keeps the $z$-conditioned $F$,
bootstraps with $a'\sim\pi_\beta$ (restoring contraction), and then performs one
BC-regularized improvement step; that recipe is validated on OGBench cube elsewhere and is
\textbf{still untested here} (queued as T8).

\subsection{Coverage reweighting, IWR, ISFB}
\emph{Coverage/IWR}: importance weights on \eqref{eq:fbloss} from state-density or
interaction statistics: 0.13--0.23. \emph{ISFB} (scalar-gate measure improvement): insert an
expectile gate inside the measure target so only high-advantage continuations propagate:
coordinate readout 0.17, AWR gate 0.15, hard gate 0.20 (torch replication 0.23). The
expectile \emph{mechanism} alone does not rescue the measure --- it is the full scalar
reward-TD coupling that matters.

\subsection{PSM and RLDP (context)}
PSM (proto successor measures) represents any policy's occupancy as an \emph{affine}
combination of policy-independent basis functions learned from Bellman-flow constraints;
our PSM-FlowBC port peaks at \textbf{0.36} (ortho-tuned, 10 seeds); RLDP at 0.17. Both
share FB's grounding problem: the basis is trained against occupancy structure, not task
value. They are the natural second target for the grounding swap (\S\ref{sec:grounding}).

\subsection{The jax graft family (structure on the working grounding)}
On the GCIQL side we grafted each FB structural element onto the scalar-TD grounding
(details and the Socratic analysis in \texttt{understanding-bilinear.tex}): bilinear head
\textbf{0.904}; $z$-conditioned $F$ 0.844; two-tower $F$ 0.864; the full stack 0.871;
FB-ortho $\lambda{=}1000$ kills it (0.072); $z$-conditioned actor \textbf{0.921}; measure as
a $\lambda\le1$ scale-normed auxiliary is free (0.86--0.90); measure-only 0.076--0.088;
the SF vector-TD family 0.00--0.16; SimpleSF (scalar TD through a trunk split) 0.38.
Conclusion, both directions: \textbf{structure survives on the scalar grounding; nothing
survives on the measure grounding.}

\section{The annealing experiments: which loss is load-bearing}\label{sec:anneal}

All four cells warm-start from the converged 0.904 bilinear-GCIQL checkpoint (optimizer
state reset; a \texttt{prepare\_anneal\_ckpt} utility rewrites \texttt{network.step} so
schedules start at zero), then train 400k further steps with a scheduled loss mix. The
schedule is linear over 300k steps, then held. Three seeds each, eval every 50k.

\begin{center}\small\begin{tabular}{lllll}
\toprule
cell & schedule & 100k & 200k--300k & endpoint read \\
\midrule
\texttt{anneal-zero} (control) & no measure; IQL only & 0.953 & 0.93--0.95 & restore valid; basin stable \\
\texttt{anneal-sgphi} & $\lambda:0\to10$ on stopgrad-$\phi$ measure & 0.929 & 0.88--0.89 & \numgood{survives at $\lambda{=}10$ (0.85)} \\
\texttt{anneal-shared} & $\lambda:0\to100$, shared legs & 0.939 & 0.256\,$\to$\,0.017 & \numbad{collapse} \\
\texttt{anneal-sgphi-iqlfade} & $\lambda:0\to10$ \emph{and} IQL weight $1\to0$ & 0.913 & 0.516\,$\to$\,0.000 & \numbad{collapse to 0} \\
\texttt{simplesf-anneal} & true-reward TD out, reward-regression in & 0.237 & 0.236\,$\to$\,0.001 & \numbad{collapse} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}\end{center}

For calibration: \emph{cold-started} stopgrad-$\phi$ measure mixing has a cliff between
$\lambda=1$ (0.86) and $\lambda=3$ (0.18), with $\lambda=10$ at 0.02. The annealed run
crosses that cliff and holds 0.85 at $\lambda=10$.

\paragraph{The three lessons.}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{Curricula cross optimization cliffs but cannot fix fixed points.} Warm-starting
plus a ramp gets the stopgrad-$\phi$ measure to a $10\times$ higher weight than any cold
start survives (\texttt{anneal-sgphi}) --- so part of the measure's damage is an
optimization-path effect. But the moment the scalar anchor is scheduled away
(\texttt{iqlfade}), the system leaves the good basin \emph{from inside it} and lands at 0.
The measure's own fixed point on cube is bad; no curriculum can change a fixed point.
\item \textbf{The scalar TD term is the load-bearing anchor, and it is cheap.} Everything
that keeps $\mathcal L_{\mathrm{IQL}}$ at full weight tolerates large measure pressure
(on the $\psi$ leg); everything that removes or fades it collapses --- including the
SimpleSF version where the replacement grounding was reward \emph{regression} rather than
reward TD (\texttt{simplesf-anneal}): a static grounding cannot hold the basin either;
the bootstrapped scalar TD specifically is what props it.
\item \textbf{Route matters: the measure hurts through $\phi$, not $\psi$.} Shared-legs
annealing collapses by $\lambda\approx30$--100 while stopgrad-$\phi$ (measure gradients
into $\psi$ only) holds at $\lambda=10$. The action-side features are the fragile asset;
the goal encoder tolerates measure shaping.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Reward grounding for successor structure: the recipe and its instances}
\label{sec:grounding}

The annealing results, the graft lattice, and the T10 telemetry jointly determine a design
recipe for making FB/PSM-style methods performant on manipulation:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{Keep the interface, swap the grounding.} The zero-shot machinery (task space
$z$, task encoder $B/\psi$, moment inference $z=\E[r\,\psi]$, $z$-conditioned policy) is
intact and cheap --- it recovered 0.955 on a scalar-grounded critic. Ground the factorization
with \emph{hindsight scalar expectile TD} (contractive; supervision concentrated on
controllable futures), not occupancy regression.
\item \textbf{Never schedule the scalar anchor away} (lesson 2). Successor-measure semantics
enter as a $\lambda\le1$ scale-normed auxiliary (free: 0.86--0.90) or as a centered objective
with its own scale (\S\ref{sec:t10}).
\item \textbf{Protect the action-side features}: if a measure-like loss is used at
$\lambda>1$, stop-gradient it into the goal leg only (lesson 3).
\item \textbf{Pin scales explicitly} when any leg lacks a bounded target: sphere-norm the
goal leg ($\|\psi\|=\sqrt d$) and give at least one leg a \emph{raw} (un-normalized)
regression --- an all-scale-normed objective is scale-invariant and inflates without bound
(measured: $Q$ to $10^{13}$; the T2 telemetry set $\E\|\psi\|^2$, $q_{\max}$ is the
early-warning instrument).
\item \textbf{Confine policy-conditioned bootstraps to a centered advantage component};
ground the baseline with a fixed-policy (SARSA) bootstrap, which is a $\gamma$-contraction.
The non-contraction of policy-conditioned measure TD (Zheng et al.\ 2026) is thereby
quarantined in the component that is small by construction.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Instance 1 --- RG-FB (built; v0 measured)}
$Q(s,a,z)=\phi(s,a)^\top z/\sqrt d$ with $z=\psi(g)$ during training, GC-IQL grounding,
flow actor conditioned on $\sg[\psi(g)]$, moment inference with matched-norm hygiene
($z\leftarrow z\cdot\overline{\|\psi(s')\|}/\|z\|$; the $\sqrt d$ convention costs
0.46). \textbf{Measured: zero-shot $0.825\pm0.014$; exact-$z$ 0.852 (inference gap 2.7
points).} The vf-conditioning knob cost $\approx$2 points on cube (the goal-agnostic anchor
is load-bearing there --- control 0.816/0.888 at 500k); the remaining gap to the 0.904/0.921
anchors is the $d{=}50$ $z$-actor combination ($d{=}512$ rerun queued). Generality:
zero-shot works for arbitrary dataset-supported goal positions (novel regions 0.672,
anchors 0.952) but not composite rewards (two-goal mixtures 0.26: the blended $z$ leaves the
trained manifold). \textbf{v1 additions}: task-mixture hindsight relabeling --- sample goals
$g_{1..K}$, weights $w$, reward $\sum_k w_k\mathbf 1[s\approx g_k]$, $z=\sum_k w_k\psi(g_k)$,
same TD --- populating the $z$-space off the single-goal manifold with grounded targets; plus
the $\lambda\le1$ measure auxiliary for honest successor-measure semantics.

\subsection{Instance 2 --- Grounded PSM (proposed)}
PSM's asset is a \emph{policy-independent} basis: occupancies are affine in
$\Phi$, $M^\pi \approx \Phi\, w_\pi + b_0$, with $\Phi$ learned from Bellman-flow
constraints. Its weakness is the same as FB's: nothing task-weighted grounds the basis.
The grounding swap: keep $\Phi(s,a)$, add a goal encoder $w(g)\in\R^d$, define
$Q(s,a,g)=\Phi(s,a)^\top w(g)/\sqrt d$, and train \emph{both} by hindsight scalar expectile
TD; retain the Bellman-flow constraint on $\Phi$ as a $\lambda\le1$ scale-normed auxiliary
(recipe items 2--3). Zero-shot inference is unchanged (solve/moment for $w$ from reward
samples). Prediction from the lattice: the scalar-grounded cell lands near bilinear-GCIQL
(the architecture differences that matter --- rank, two-tower vs monolithic --- are worth
$<6$ points); the informative comparison is whether PSM's flow-constrained basis buys
anything over RG-FB's free $\phi$ (better composite-reward generalization is the plausible
win, testable with the T6 probe).

\subsection{Instance 3 --- Centered, anchored measure (T10; running)}\label{sec:t10}
The measure-native version of the recipe: give the action-contingent component its own
grounded objective. $b(s)$ tracks the behavior-mean feature ($K{=}8$ flow samples,
scale-normed regression); $\Delta M = (\phi-\sg b)^\top\tilde\psi/\sqrt d$ with
$\tilde\psi=\sqrt d\,\psi/\|\psi\|$;
\begin{equation}
\mathcal L =
\underbrace{\mathcal L_{\mathrm{meas}}(M,\,a'{=}a'_{\mathrm{data}})}_{\text{raw, SARSA base leg}}
+ \lambda\,\underbrace{\mathcal L_{\Delta M}(a'{\sim}\pi)}_{\text{scale-normed}}
+ \mathcal L_b + \mathcal L_\perp(\tilde\psi),
\end{equation}
actor on the full $Q$ normalized by $\sg|Q_{\mathrm{full}}|$.
\textbf{Status (2026-07-16)}: as an \emph{auxiliary} on the scalar grounding (T10-a):
healthy, 0.71--0.78 mid-training --- the first FB-style loss that does not hurt on cube.
As the \emph{sole} grounding (T10-b2, anchored): \numgood{0.221 mean @50k across 3 seeds
(0.232/0.176/0.256)} --- already $\approx 3\times$ the historical measure-only ceiling
(0.076--0.088); pre-registered read at 500k: $\ge$0.5 = a real measure-native rescue,
0.3--0.5 = partial (residual attributed to missing counterfactual support in $\rho$:
17 mid-air drops per 1M transitions), $<$0.2 = dead.

\subsection{Instance 4 --- what the annealing curriculum is still good for}
Annealing cannot replace grounding (lesson 1), but it remains the right tool for
\emph{constraint injection}: any structural pressure with a cold-start cliff (ortho, measure
shaping of $\psi$, quantization, sparsity) can be ramped onto a warm scalar-grounded model at
$10\times$ the cold-start-tolerable weight. The open curriculum question worth one run: ramp
the \emph{centered} (not raw) measure onto the warm basin with the anchor kept --- if
T10-b2 plateaus below 0.5, this hybrid is the next cell.
