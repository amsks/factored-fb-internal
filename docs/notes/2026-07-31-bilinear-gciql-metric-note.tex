\paragraph{Setup.}
Goal-conditioned MDP with states $\mathcal{S}$, deterministic dynamics (as in OGBench cube given full state), sparse reward $r(s;g) = \mathbf{1}[s{=}g] - 1$, termination at the goal, discount $\gamma$. Let $d^*(s,g) \in \{0,1,\dots,\infty\}$ be the minimal number of steps from $s$ to $g$ (over in-support policies, for offline data). The optimal goal-conditioned value is
\begin{equation}
V^*(s,g) \;=\; -\frac{1-\gamma^{d^*(s,g)}}{1-\gamma}, \qquad \gamma^{\infty} := 0 .
\label{eq:vstar}
\end{equation}
$d^*$ is a \emph{quasimetric}: $d^*(s,s)=0$ and $d^*(s,g) \le d^*(s,w) + d^*(w,g)$, with symmetry \emph{not} required (Wang et al.\ 2023, Thm.~1: quasimetrics are \emph{exactly} the class of optimal goal-reaching values). Bilinear GCIQL parametrizes $V(s,g) = \ip{\varphi(s)}{\psi(g)}/\sqrt{d}$ and $Q(s,a,g)=\ip{\varphi(s,a)}{\psi(g)}/\sqrt{d}$, trained with expectile TD; as the expectile $\tau \to 1$, $V \to V^*$ over in-support actions. Throughout, ``the critic learns a metric'' is made precise via three claims that we can now state as identities.

\section{The negated value is itself a quasimetric}

\begin{proposition}[Discounted quasimetric]\label{prop:qm}
Let $\phi_\gamma(x) := \frac{1-\gamma^{x}}{1-\gamma}$. Then $D_\gamma := -V^* = \phi_\gamma \circ d^*$ is a bounded quasimetric ($D_\gamma \le \tfrac{1}{1-\gamma}$).
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
$\phi_\gamma$ is increasing, concave, and $\phi_\gamma(0)=0$; hence subadditive, $\phi_\gamma(a+b)\le\phi_\gamma(a)+\phi_\gamma(b)$. So
$\phi_\gamma(d^*(s,g)) \le \phi_\gamma(d^*(s,w)+d^*(w,g)) \le \phi_\gamma(d^*(s,w))+\phi_\gamma(d^*(w,g))$, and $D_\gamma(s,g)=0 \iff d^*(s,g)=0$. (Metric-preserving transforms; the same argument appears implicitly in Wang et al.\ 2023.)
\end{proof}

So bilinear GCIQL's training target is \emph{already} a quasimetric --- no log-transform needed. Equivalently, define the \textbf{discounted reachability kernel}
\begin{equation}
K^*(s,g) := \gamma^{\,d^*(s,g)} \;=\; 1 + (1-\gamma)\,V^*(s,g) \;\in\; [0,1].
\label{eq:kernel}
\end{equation}

\begin{corollary}[Multiplicative transitivity]\label{cor:mult}
$K^*(s,s)=1$ and $K^*(s,g) \;\ge\; K^*(s,w)\,K^*(w,g)$ for all $s,w,g$ (immediate from the triangle inequality and $\gamma<1$).
\end{corollary}

\section{Bilinear GCIQL $=$ low-rank factorization of $K^*$}

Substituting the bilinear form into \eqref{eq:kernel}: with $\tilde\varphi(s) := \big(1,\; \tfrac{1-\gamma}{\sqrt d}\varphi(s)\big)$ and $\tilde\psi(g) := \big(1,\; \psi(g)\big)$,
\begin{equation}
K(s,g) \;=\; \ip{\tilde\varphi(s)}{\tilde\psi(g)}, \qquad \operatorname{rank} \le d+1 .
\label{eq:factorization}
\end{equation}
\emph{Bilinear GCIQL succeeds if and only if the discounted reachability kernel $\gamma^{d^*}$ is approximately rank-$d$.} This is a statement about the spectrum of the environment's temporal geometry, not about the algorithm: the observed latent-dimension cliff on cube (success $9.6\%$ at $d{=}5$, $\sim 90\%$ at $d{=}50$) reads off the numerical rank of $K^*$ restricted to the data manifold. Note $\gamma$ acts as a bandwidth: $K^* = e^{-\beta d^*}$ with $\beta = \ln(1/\gamma)$, so larger $\gamma$ flattens the kernel and lowers its effective rank.

\section{Contrastive RL: same kernel, log link, behavior policy}

The CRL critic $f(s,a,g)=\ip{\varphi(s,a)}{\psi(g)}$ satisfies at the binary-NCE optimum (Eysenbach et al.\ 2022, Lem.~4.1) $\exp f^* = p^{\pi}_\gamma(s_+{=}g \mid s,a)/p(g)$, the discounted-occupancy ratio. Under deterministic dynamics, if the behavior trajectory from $s$ first (and only) visits $g$ at step $T^{\pi}(s,g)$, then $p^{\pi}_\gamma(g|s) = (1-\gamma)\gamma^{T^{\pi}(s,g)}$, hence
\begin{equation}
f^*(s,g) \;=\; -\ln\!\tfrac{1}{\gamma}\; T^{\pi}(s,g) \;+\; c(g), \qquad c(g) = \ln(1-\gamma) - \ln p(g).
\label{eq:crl}
\end{equation}
Comparing \eqref{eq:factorization} and \eqref{eq:crl}: \textbf{both methods fit a rank-$d$ bilinear model to the same family of kernels $\gamma^{\,T(s,g)}$, but under different links and different policies} --- CRL factorizes $\log K^{\pi} + c(g)$ (log link, \emph{behavior}-policy hitting time, per-goal offset), GCIQL factorizes $K^*$ affinely (identity link, \emph{optimal} hitting time, no offset). Myers et al.\ (2024) recover a quasimetric from occupancy critics by self-subtraction, $d_{\mathrm{SD}}(s,g) := f(g,g)-f(s,g)$, which cancels $c(g)$. Applied to GCIQL's logits, where $V^*(g,g)=0$, self-subtraction returns $-V^* = D_\gamma$: \emph{for bilinear GCIQL the Myers construction and the raw negated value coincide}, and Prop.~\ref{prop:qm} says the result is a quasimetric exactly when the value is optimal. (FB representations sit in the same family one level up: $F^\top B$ factorizes the successor \emph{measure} whose $g$-marginal ratio is \eqref{eq:crl}; GCIQL's $K^*$ is the optimal-policy, sparse-reward special case.)

\section{VIP: the symmetric--PSD case is a Euclidean embedding (Schoenberg)}

VIP hard-codes $V(s;g) = -\lVert \phi(s)-\phi(g)\rVert_2$ and trains $\phi$ so that embedding distance equals a (KL-regularized) optimal value. Bilinear GCIQL is connected to this by a purely linear-algebraic fact. Suppose the learned factorization is (approximately) \emph{symmetric PSD} on the data manifold, i.e.\ $K(s,g)=\ip{u(s)}{u(g)}$ for a single map $u$ (equivalently: the symmetric part of $K$ dominates and is PSD; $u$ exists by Mercer). Then, using $K(s,s) = 1 \iff V(s,s)=0$, polarization gives
\begin{equation}
\big\lVert u(s) - u(g) \big\rVert_2^2 \;=\; K(s,s) + K(g,g) - 2K(s,g) \;=\; 2\big(1-\gamma^{\,d^*(s,g)}\big) \;=\; 2(1-\gamma)\,\big({-}V^*(s,g)\big).
\label{eq:polarization}
\end{equation}
\textbf{A symmetric-PSD bilinear GCIQL solution is automatically a VIP-style embedding}: squared Euclidean distance in factor space equals $2(1-\gamma)$ times the negated value (VIP uses $\lVert\cdot\rVert$, GCIQL implies $\lVert\cdot\rVert^2$; since $\sqrt{\cdot}$ is concave with $\sqrt 0=0$, $\lVert u(s)-u(g)\rVert = \sqrt{2(1-\gamma)D_\gamma}$ is again a metric-preserving transform, so $u$ is a genuine metric embedding into $\R^{d+1}$). Schoenberg's theorem supplies the converse characterization: $e^{-\beta d}$ is PSD for all $\beta>0$ iff $d$ is conditionally negative definite (of \emph{negative type}, i.e.\ Hilbert-embeddable). Hence: \emph{bilinear GCIQL can realize a symmetric-PSD solution at every $\gamma$ iff the environment's temporal metric is of negative type} --- and the antisymmetric part of the learned $K$ is precisely the obstruction (measured by probe P4 of the experiment spec). Finally, if norms concentrate, $\lVert u \rVert \approx c$, then \eqref{eq:polarization} gives $V \approx c^2\big(\cos\angle(u(s),u(g)) - 1\big)/(1-\gamma)$ up to constants: \emph{``GCIQL learns cosine similarity'' is exactly the symmetric-PSD-plus-norm-concentration special case} (probe P5).

\section{QRL: same target class, complementary parametrization}

QRL learns $d_\theta \approx d^*$ directly, with the quasimetric axioms enforced \emph{architecturally} (IQE head) and a constrained-maximization objective replacing TD. Prop.~\ref{prop:qm} shows bilinear GCIQL aims at (a bounded transform of) the \emph{same target class} but from the opposite side: the bilinear form is unconstrained --- low-rank projection does not preserve the multiplicative transitivity of Cor.~\ref{cor:mult} --- so nothing forces $\hat K$ to be a legal reachability kernel off the training distribution. This yields a ground-truth-free diagnostic:

\begin{proposition}[Suboptimality certificate]\label{prop:cert}
Let $\hat d(s,g) := \log_\gamma \hat K(s,g)$ with $\hat K = 1 + (1-\gamma)\hat V$ clipped to $(0,1]$. If $\hat V$ equals the optimal value of \emph{any} goal-reaching MDP, then $\hat d$ has zero triangle-inequality violations. Hence any measured violation mass (probe P3) certifies $\hat V \notin \{V^* : \text{goal-reaching MDPs}\}$, without access to ground-truth distances.
\end{proposition}

\section{Summary and testable predictions}

One object organizes all four methods: the reachability kernel $K = e^{-\beta d}$ of a quasimetric $d$.
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{l l l l}
\hline
Method & Fits & Link & Policy / constraint \\
\hline
Bilinear GCIQL & $K^* = \gamma^{d^*}$ & identity (affine) & optimal (expectile); unconstrained rank $d$ \\
CRL & $\log K^{\pi} + c(g)$ & log & behavior; unconstrained rank $d$ \\
VIP & $-\phi_\gamma(d^*)$ (KL-reg.) & $-\lVert\phi(s)-\phi(g)\rVert$ & optimal (dual); symmetric by construction \\
QRL & $d^*$ & identity & optimal; quasimetric axioms architectural \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The identities are falsifiable, and map one-to-one onto the probe battery of the companion spec (\texttt{docs/specs/2026-07-31-bilinear-gciql-metric-probe.md}): \eqref{eq:factorization} $\Rightarrow$ spectrum/effective-rank of $\hat K$ vs.\ latent-dim cliff (P7); \eqref{eq:crl} $\Rightarrow$ rank-agreement between GCIQL's $-\hat V$ and CRL's $f(g,g)-f(s,g)$ (P6); \eqref{eq:polarization} $\Rightarrow$ if the learned solution is near-symmetric-PSD, then $\lVert u(s)-u(g)\rVert^2$ (equivalently $\psi$-space geometry) must rank-match $-\hat V$ and VIP's distance (P4--P6), with norm concentration deciding the cosine special case (P5); Prop.~\ref{prop:cert} $\Rightarrow$ triangle-violation mass of $\hat d$ upper-bounds how ``value-like'' the critic is (P3).

\paragraph{Caveats.} \eqref{eq:vstar}--\eqref{eq:crl} are exact for deterministic dynamics and single-visit trajectories; stochasticity replaces $\gamma^{d^*}$ by $\E[\gamma^{T}]$ and hitting-time logic by occupancies (cube is deterministic given full state, so the identities apply up to data support). Expectile $\tau=0.9$ approximates but does not attain the in-support max, and $V(g,g)=0$ holds at the fixed point but is not enforced --- the self-value $\hat V(s,s)$ is itself a cheap probe of how far training got. $d^*$ is reachability \emph{within the data support}, not the physical MDP.
