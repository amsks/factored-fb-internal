# Play data (TD-MPC2 / scripted-oracle) — the 9 non-cube dev tasks

Generated by `collect_play.py` (launcher: `run_play_collection.sh`) to test whether the
goal-conditioned critics (esp. `iql_bilinear`/biql) work on diverse *play* data the way they
do on OGBench cube-single, rather than on our narrow expert demos.

## play-resample (diverse-goal, continuous) — `collect_play_resample.py`

OGBench-style diverse-GOAL play: one long continuous episode chains many goals (reach → hold
~1s → resample a valid goal, not in a wall, not too close → continue, no reset). Goal-relabeling
then sees a broad goal distribution *within* a trajectory — the property biql/gciql need, that
action-noise (play-noise) can't produce.

Only works where the TD-MPC2 checkpoint is genuinely goal-conditioned (goal varied in training,
so the policy follows the obs goal). A redirection gate auto-skips fixed-goal policies.

**896k transitions, 20.0k chained goals, 6 tasks** (4 Box2D + 2 ManiSkill).
Data at `.../collected_play_resample/`. ManiSkill uses `collect_play_resample_ms.py` (moves the
SAPIEN goal actor: push-cube `goal_region`, pick-cube `goal_site`).

**Feasibility (Box2D goal-ctrl + ManiSkill; Metaworld excluded):** center-ctrl / center-wall / maze Box2D
policies were trained to a *fixed* objective and ignore the obs goal (gate skips them). Metaworld
envs are fixed-goal-per-episode (obs doesn't track a mid-episode target change). ManiSkill works via K single-env instances (newt forces num_envs=1) whose SAPIEN goal actor is
moved mid-episode. Metaworld is excluded: its obs doesn't reliably track a mid-episode goal
change (y-coord env-controlled). Both datasets on HF: `TommyShen/factored-fb-play`.
