At the door $D$, the true futures of crossing and failing to cross differ sharply: crossing spreads probability mass into the next room while failing leaves the agent stuck at the doorway. FB's fit assigns both actions a nearly identical, diffuse future, so its critic barely prefers the precise crossing action over a failed one, and this margin collapses further as the failed crossing becomes more persistent. Density-FB keeps the two futures visually and numerically distinct, and keeps the margin between them close to the truth at every stickiness level tested. Each column shares one color scale between its cross and fail rows (fixed to the max of the two, per estimator), so the difference between the rows is a genuine change in the estimator's occupancy prediction, not a rescaling artifact.
