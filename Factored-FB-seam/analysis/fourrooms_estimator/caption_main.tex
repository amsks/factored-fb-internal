This figure shows the true, FB, and Density successor-measure rows for the directed policy from $S_0$ toward $G$ (top), the FB and Density rows for crossing versus failing to cross the sticky doorway $D$ (bottom left), and the normalized action gap at $D$ versus the stickiness parameter $k$ at rank $d=8$ (bottom right; FB's negative entries are clipped to 0 for display). A gap of 1 means the fit matches the true value difference between crossing and failing to cross; a gap of 0 means the fit cannot tell the two actions apart, and FB's gap falls toward 0 as the doorway gets stickier while Density's stays high.
