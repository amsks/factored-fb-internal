\vspace{4pt}
\parbox{\textwidth}{\footnotesize
\textbf{Three readings.} (i) Density-FB is the only method that works at state input
($0.91$ on \texttt{cube-single}, $4\times$ the best retrofit), so transplanting the
categorical estimator into RLDP/PSM/HILP does \emph{not} reproduce it --- and the missing
ingredient is not the object coordinates. (ii) Object coordinates rescue the retrofits on
\texttt{cube-single} ($3$--$8\times$) but move Density-FB only $0.91 \to 0.94$; on
\texttt{cube-double} the reverse holds --- Density-FB needs them ($0.05 \to 0.64$) while the
retrofits reach only ${\sim}0.2$. (iii) HILP is inert to both.

\smallskip
$^{\ast}$\textbf{Provisional, and still unexplained.} The FB row runs
\texttt{ortho\_coef}$=1.0$ (the value our yaml copied from td\_jepa's OGBench
\texttt{BASE\_CFG}), which a line-by-line audit identified as the sole divergence from the
torch implementation --- the objective, $z$-law and actor all transcribe faithfully, and
td\_jepa's \texttt{sweep\_cube} sweeps $\{100,1000\}$, never $1.0$. Rerunning at
\texttt{ortho\_coef}$=1000$ raises \texttt{cube-single} from $0.11$ to
$\mathbf{0.203\pm0.046}$ ($n{=}3$) --- real, but far short of the $0.42$--$0.53$ of the
reference stack and the $0.520\pm0.055$ of \emph{Is Manipulation Hard for Successor
Measures?}, and on \texttt{cube-double} it gives $0.000$. So \texttt{ortho\_coef} was one
cause and not the cause. Both FB columns above are at $1.0$, so the pair is internally
matched; a matched \texttt{ortho\_coef}$=1000$ pair has not been run. Note also that the
comparison targets evaluate at 1M steps against this table's 500k.
$^{\dagger}$Read at 450k: the cell died at 465k with
\texttt{CUDA\_ERROR\_STREAM\_CAPTURE\_INVALIDATED} (the known stream-capture fault; CUDA
graphs are disabled in later waves). ``---'' marks a cell that was never run.
