RLDP at ortho 1 (exp/ortho1) is the configuration used everywhere else in this paper: it is RLDP's own published default, and it is worth scene $4.7\to10.2$ and puzzle $2.4\to10.4$ over TD-JEPA's grid value. Higher coefficients than 1 are worse on every task.
Reading: FB and HILP reproduce to within a few points on every task (FB 15.2 vs 17.5 on average, HILP 30.7 vs 35.2); RLDP reproduces only once its own orthonormality coefficient is restored (12.7 vs 12.5), and puzzle-3x3 is where our RLDP stays furthest below the published number (10.4 vs 14.6).
\textbf{avg} is the mean of the four rows, recomputed per column; no spread is carried through the recomputation.
On cube-double, reward inference is capped near half of goal-conditioned success for every method: OGBench re-permutes the cube-to-target assignment at every reset, while the reward samples used to infer $z$ encode one fixed assignment.
