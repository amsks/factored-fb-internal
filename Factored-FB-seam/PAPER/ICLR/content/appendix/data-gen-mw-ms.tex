\section{MetaWorld and ManiSkill datasets}
\label{app:data-mw-ms}

This appendix describes how the offline datasets used in Section~\ref{sec:exp-manipulation} were collected.
The same environment wrapper is used for data collection and for evaluation, so the observation and action spaces, the control frequency, and the success criterion are identical in both.

\paragraph{Common protocol.}
Each dataset consists of $N$ fixed-length trajectories of $T$ environment steps, giving $N \times T \geq 10^6$ transitions per task (Table~\ref{tab:app-data-mw-ms}).
Every trajectory pursues a single goal drawn once at reset from the environment's own goal distribution; the goal is never re-drawn within a trajectory.
The behavior policy typically completes the task early and then holds its final configuration, so the remainder of the trajectory records the object at the goal.
The fraction of transitions in which the object remains at the goal (the at-goal fraction) ranges from $0.76$ to $0.95$ across tasks; this is the persistent-occupancy structure discussed in Section~\ref{sec:failure}.
Each dataset is stored as one packed array file per task with fields \texttt{observation}, \texttt{action}, \texttt{next\_observation}, \texttt{reward}, \texttt{terminal} (all zero; the horizon is fixed), \texttt{timeout} (set at $t = T-1$), and \texttt{success}; MetaWorld additionally stores \texttt{achieved\_goal} and \texttt{goal\_switch}.
Actions are clipped to $[-1,1]$.

\paragraph{MetaWorld (\texttt{push}, \texttt{bin-picking}, \texttt{box-close}).}
We use the goal-observable V2 environments behind a wrapper replicating the collection setup of \citet{hansen-iclr26a}: action repeat $2$ with summed rewards, a reset consisting of \texttt{env.reset()} followed by one zero-action step (which fills the previous-frame half of the $39$-dimensional state), and $4$-dimensional actions.
The environment's internal path-length truncation is disabled so that every trajectory runs for the full $T = 500$ wrapper steps ($1000$ simulator steps).
Each task has $N = 2010$ trajectories ($1{,}005{,}000$ transitions), collected in $30$ shards of $67$ episodes with per-episode seeding of the global random number generator, from which MetaWorld draws its reset randomization.

The behavior policies are the scripted oracles shipped with MetaWorld.
For \texttt{push} we use \texttt{SawyerPushV2Policy}, which follows the goal channel of the observation.
For the two fixture tasks, the transport policies (\texttt{SawyerPickPlaceV2Policy} for \texttt{bin-picking}, \texttt{SawyerBoxCloseV2Policy} for \texttt{box-close}) are augmented with a deterministic grasp primitive that repairs the scripted grasp under action repeat $2$: the gripper is aligned above the object to within $1.5$--$2$\,cm with hysteresis, descends open to the grasp depth (the mocap floor at $z \approx 0.065$ for the cube; $5$\,cm above the lid for the handle), closes, and hands control back to the scripted policy once the finger separation enters the measured carry band ($0.35$--$0.60$ for the cube, $0.25$--$0.40$ for the lid).
Per-episode Gaussian action noise is added with standard deviation $\sigma \sim \mathcal U[0, \sigma_{\max}]$, $\sigma_{\max} = 0.02$ for \texttt{push} and \texttt{bin-picking} and $0.01$ for \texttt{box-close}.

The goal protocol is task-specific.
\texttt{push}: one target per episode from the environment's own reset distribution (goal-space $xy$ with $z$ drawn from the empirical pool of reset heights).
\texttt{bin-picking}: one placement into the goal bin, after which the object is held in the closed gripper.
\texttt{box-close}: one placement of the lid onto the box, after which the lid is released and the hand hovers above it (holding the lid with the gripper closed makes the scripted controller oscillate).
Fixtures are restored to their canonical pose at every reset and clamped to a wall-safe region (box $y \in [0.68, 0.78]$).
Because the environment clips the goal dimensions of the observation (indices $36$--$38$) to its goal-space box, which is degenerate for the fixture tasks, the true target is written into those dimensions on every stored frame.
Success is $\|\mathrm{obj} - \mathrm{target}\| < r$ with $r = 0.06$, $0.06$, and $0.08$\,m for the three tasks.
Trajectories in which the object leaves the workspace (\texttt{push}: $|x| > 0.30$; \texttt{bin-picking}: $|x| > 0.45$; \texttt{box-close}: $|x| > 0.38$ or $y \notin [0.35, 0.90]$) are rejected at the first offending step and re-rolled, with at most $8$ retries.
The measured at-goal fractions are $0.921$, $0.895$, and $0.812$, and no rejected trajectory was released into the datasets.

\paragraph{ManiSkill (\texttt{push-cube}, \texttt{pick-cube}, \texttt{stack-cube}, \texttt{poke-cube}, \texttt{lift-peg}).}
We use the ManiSkill3 (version 3.0.1) state-observation environments with the per-task control modes of \citet{hansen-iclr26a} (\texttt{pd\_ee\_delta\_pos} for \texttt{pick-cube}, \texttt{pd\_ee\_delta\_pose} otherwise), wrapped with \texttt{CPUGymWrapper(ignore\_terminations=True)}.
The horizon is overridden to $T = 125$ steps, five times the $25$-step evaluation horizon, so that the post-completion hold is recorded.
Each task has $N = 8000$ trajectories ($1{,}000{,}000$ transitions), collected in $8$ shards of $1000$ episodes with \texttt{env.reset(seed)} seeding.

The behavior policies are the released multitask TD-MPC2 checkpoints of \citet{hansen-iclr26a} (model size B), with observations zero-padded to $128$ dimensions and the $16$-dimensional action head truncated to the task's action dimension.
Actions are drawn from the stochastic policy head (\texttt{eval\_mode=False}), with planning disabled (\texttt{mpc=False}) and no additional exploration noise or $\epsilon$-mixing.
The environment's own goal distribution is used unchanged.
Tasks complete in roughly $10$--$20$ steps and the policy then holds.
The measured episode success rates are $0.997$, $0.990$, $0.940$, $0.948$, and $0.921$, and the at-goal fractions are $0.949$, $0.906$, $0.756$, $0.858$, and $0.783$.
